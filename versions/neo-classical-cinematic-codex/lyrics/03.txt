[Intro: wet clay turns with harp, water, and one human heartbeat][Intro][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, inward and wondering]
I am Prometheus,
the Foreseer.

I have seen every tomorrow
of this finished world.

The world was finished.
But not complete.

The mountains do not wonder.
The rivers do not ask.
The gods ask only whose—
whose throne,
whose sky,
whose flame.

Every tomorrow is the same day.

[Pre-Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, purpose widening]
Here by the river
the clay lies open.

I will shape the one tomorrow
I cannot see:
a mind that is not mine,
a will I have not written,
a question with no answer waiting.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, long ascending line]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
I will open one tomorrow
and not close my hand within.

[Verse 2][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, tender craft aria]
From river clay
I shape your hands.
I give you eyes for dawn.
I give you thought
to meet the world
when every map is gone.

Awaken, humanity.
Your future is not mine.

[HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, warm breath resonance, newly awake and carefully spaced]
Light…
moving through the leaves.

Sound…
inside of me.

Prometheus,
my maker—

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, warm and clear]
Your maker—
and no more your master
than the river is.

[HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, warm breath resonance, newly awake and carefully spaced]
what will we become?

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, long ascending line]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
I will open one tomorrow
and not close my hand within.

[Breakdown: silence; one shared heartbeat]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, astonishment breaking into joy]
I do not know!

I—who have seen
every ending—
I do not know!

At last, at last—
a tomorrow
no eye has read!

You are not finished.
You are becoming.
Your answer is your own.

[HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, warm breath resonance, stronger and curious]
Then stay near enough to teach us—
and far enough to let us grow.

[Instrumental Break: solo violin and cello circle the heartbeat without resolving]

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS AND HUMANITY — Male high tenor and female intimate alto, contrasting resonance, radiant duet]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
We will open one tomorrow
and not close our hands within.

[Outro][HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, warm breath resonance, fading into first night]
What will we become?

[End](Cold night gathers; distant human murmurs answer.)
