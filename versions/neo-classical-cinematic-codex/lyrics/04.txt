[Intro: distant human murmurs gather over low strings and empty air][Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed low chamber choir, cold chest resonance, questions entering one by one]
Who shaped these hands?
Who woke these eyes?
Who left us breathing
under lightless skies?

Prometheus—
maker—
show your face.

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, exposed and steady]
I shaped you.
I am here.

[HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed low chamber choir, cold chest resonance, accumulating counterpoint]
You gave us eyes.
Where is the dawn?
You gave us hands.
They close on wind.
You gave us questions.
The dark consumes each one.

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed low chamber choir, tightening chest resonance, forceful homophony]
The night has teeth.
The frost has hands.
Our maker stands
while heaven warms.

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed full chamber choir, thunderous layered resonance, accusing]
Do not wake a mind,
then wall the world away.
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—
you owe it light.

[Verse 2][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, fighting to be heard]
I gave you all
I was allowed to give—

[HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed low chamber choir, sharp chest resonance, interrupting]
Allowed by whom?
Who chains the maker?
Who keeps the flame?

Why shape a heart
and leave it to the frost?
Why make a mind
and teach it only loss?

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed full chamber choir, thunderous layered resonance, accusing]
Do not wake a mind,
then wall the world away.
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—
you owe it light.

[Breakdown: the choir cuts to silence; one crowd breath]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, wounded and unbowed]
Why create
what fear can break?

Because the answer
must be made.

I hear the debt inside your cry.
I will not give you questions
and close the world away.

[Instrumental Break: solo cello repeats the accusation; rising violin answers]

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed full chamber choir with high tenor above, vast layered resonance, final accusation]
Do not wake a mind,
then wall the world away.
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—
you owe it light.

[Outro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed low chamber choir, cold breath resonance, receding]
We have the questions.
We have no spark.

[End](One anvil tone; an eagle cries across the cold wind.)
