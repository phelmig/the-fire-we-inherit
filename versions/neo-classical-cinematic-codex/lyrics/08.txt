[Intro: the whispered “Open” fractures into funeral bell, chain pulse, and racing strings][Intro][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, vulnerable chest resonance, urgent and tightening]
I came among humanity
with gold at my throat,
a road I had not chosen,
and a vessel that breathed.

They welcomed me to fire.
No hand below had forged the snare.

They said, "Do not listen."
They said, "Do not see."

Then why build a questioning mind
and place the question inside me?

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Mixed chamber choir, panicked layered resonance, rapid fragments]
Hunger.
Fever.
Grief.
Despair.
Guard the grain.
Bar the gate.
Fear the stranger.
Name the blame.

[Chorus][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, open chest resonance, broad defiant lament]
Fear may ring the bell.
Fear may name the storm.
But greed will build a throne from it
and call that prison warm.
What was sent to warn the living
must not rule the human mind.

[Verse 2][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, breaking chest resonance, horrified]
I raised the seal.

Sorrow flew.
Envy learned the watchful eye.
Loss drew borders through the hand.
Fear promised safety
if the living would obey.

I did not make these curses.
I did not fill the snare.
The hands that sealed the grief
sent me below to wear their shame.

I opened a door—
but who had filled the flame?

Do not make me
the face of your shame.
The hands that built the trap
must answer in their name.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, exact and protective]
Pandora,
you are not the curse.

The price was set
before your question.
No hand that builds the trap
may blame the wounded first.
