# The Fire We Inherit — Original (Development) Version

The heterogeneous styles as they evolved track by track during writing. Each
track's sound was designed individually for its dramatic moment — duel worlds
in 05, the accusation opera of 04, the storm-start ring of 10 — with no
album-wide sonic unification. Preserved as the source of per-track musical
detail and as the baseline the unified versions were built from.

Styles live in `styles/NN.txt`; lyrics come from the repo-root `songs/`.
