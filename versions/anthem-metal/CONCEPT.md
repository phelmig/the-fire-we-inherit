# The Fire We Inherit — Anthem Metal Version

The album rendered as sacral anthemic power metal — church-organ majesty,
military snare, galloping riffs, and massed gang-chant choruses. Where the
epic-metal version is a dark symphonic opera, this version is a battle hymnal:
every chorus is a creed the crowd can sing back.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor (every track):** "Sacral anthemic power metal opera — *[track
  flavor]*, *[tempo/key]*."
- **Shared palette:** galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless
  double-kick, military snare, sacral church organ, synth-brass fanfares,
  bells, and massed anthemic gang choirs — "the album's shared stage."
- **Production signature (identical on every track):** "Epic studio quality,
  professional mastering, punchy drums, massive singalong choirs, bright
  powerful anthem mix — one album, one sound."

## Rules

- Each track keeps its dramatic identity: tempo, key, cast declaration, and
  per-section performance directions come from the track, not the version.
- **Quiet chapters (00, 01, 11, 12, 14):** carry the restraint line — the
  sacral organ and hymnal choir carry the identity while the drums and riffs
  fall away.
- Refrains lean anthem: gang chant, call-and-answer, victory-oath singalongs —
  but the story's dark refrains (07, 09) stay liturgical and grim, never
  festive.
- Styles live in `styles/NN.txt`; lyrics always come from the repo-root
  `songs/` directory (single source of truth across versions).

## Risks to watch

Anthemic gang choruses tipping tragic scenes (08, 09) into party-metal
triumph; the sacral organ flattening the quiet chapters into church kitsch;
the awakening blooms (03, 05) reading as festival singalong instead of earned
wonder.
