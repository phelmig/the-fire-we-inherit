Sacral anthemic power metal opera — spoken overture, 60 BPM, D minor.
Galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, military snare, sacral church organ, synth-brass fanfares, bells, and massed anthemic gang choirs — the album's shared stage.
Restraint: the shared metal stage falls almost silent here — one of the album's quiet chapters, its weight implied, not played.
Choir: wordless sacral female voices over hymn-slow church organ, swelling gradually beneath the voice.
Narration: dry, intimate, commanding — spoken, unhurried.
Duel: the organ argues for control, one ascending violin for creation — trading phrases beneath the question, unresolved.
Finale: massed voices declaim once like a creed, then thunder.
Ending: unresolved beneath rising distant thunder.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy drums, massive singalong choirs, bright powerful anthem mix — one album, one sound.
