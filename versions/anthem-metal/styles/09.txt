Sacral anthemic power metal opera — oppressive march of law, 76 accelerating toward 116 BPM, E minor.
Galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, military snare, sacral church organ, synth-brass fanfares, bells, and massed anthemic gang choirs — the album's shared stage.
Broken bells, piano, cello, low organ, slow toms and wind, then anvils, rising strings, galloping riffs, brass, mechanical percussion, relentless military snare and double-kick.
Opening: mournful, quiet.
Build: the machinery grows colder and more relentless as it accelerates into a parade-ground march.
Litany choir: rigid, chanted, cutting off a bright urgent tenor.
Climax: vast and oppressive, not triumphant.
Ending: sudden emptiness — wind over a cold forge, one faint ember pulse.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy drums, massive singalong choirs, bright powerful anthem mix — one album, one sound.
