Sacral anthemic power metal opera — massed accusation scene, D minor, building from murmur to storm.
Galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, military snare, sacral church organ, synth-brass fanfares, bells, and massed anthemic gang choirs — the album's shared stage.
A powerful accusing chorus slowly rising against one lone bright tenor — a crowd versus a single man.
The chorus begins immediately as a circling murmur, swells in marching waves over military snare, and breaks into a massed chanted wall; after the wall, a sub-drop into dead silence; the lone tenor answers, clear and unbowed.
Ending: the crowd recedes to a hushed murmur; an eagle cries; the lone voice remains, quiet and resolved.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy drums, massive singalong choirs, bright powerful anthem mix — one album, one sound.
