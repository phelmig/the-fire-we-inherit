[Intro: the thunder from the prologue dissolves into a primordial drone]

[Verse 1][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, nearly spoken, exact and reverent]
In the beginning—

was Chaos.

I am Athena.

I did not witness
the first dawn.

I learned its shape
from the wounds
each throne passed on.

No sky above.
No earth below.
No law to bind
what could not grow.

[PRIMORDIAL CHOIR — Low mixed choir, distant and formless]
Chaos.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, measured and austere]
Then earth took form.
Then sky was drawn.
Then Uranus raised
the first throne before dawn.

He gave the world
its shape and name—
then feared the life
the earth became.

[BURIED CHOIR — Low male choir, rigid and descending]
Under stone.
Under sky.
What is born
must never rise.

Close the gate!
Seal the dawn!
Bury the future
before it is born!

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, restrained intensity]
But what is buried
learns the weight
of every wall,
of every gate.

Kronos broke
the elder chain.
The prison opened.
The sky knew pain.

[TITAN CHOIR — Low mixed choir, forceful and rising]
Break the gate!
Unseal the stone!
No buried child
shall feed a throne!

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, darkening]
He took the crown.
He named it peace.
Then heard that one
would end his reign.

The gate swung shut.
The feast began.
He swallowed each tomorrow
before tomorrow ran.

[TITAN CHOIR — Low male choir, slow and devouring]
Close the gate.
Seal the age.
Keep every heir
inside the cage.

[Breakdown: one heartbeat beneath mountain wind.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, intimate and grave]
But one child vanished
from the hunger of the throne.

No herald spoke.
No court was told.

Only mountain dark,
a heartbeat,
and shields against the stone.

[HIDDEN CHOIR — Treble and low voices, hushed behind shield strikes]
Hide the breath.
Drown the cry.
Let the nameless
child survive.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, almost whispered]
His name was Zeus.

He learned the world
behind a closed gate.

He learned what fear
will make of fate.

[One distant pulse of thunder.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, alone]
In the beginning
was Chaos.

Then power
learned to fear
what came next.

[End]
