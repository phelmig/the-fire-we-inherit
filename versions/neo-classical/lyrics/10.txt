[Intro][ATHENA — Female dark contralto, low and accelerating, relentless]
Each age turned faster.

A century.
A decade.
A year.

The sealing and the breaking
blurred into one burning wheel.

[KEEPERS' CHORUS — Urgent whisper inside the storm]
Pass the ember!
Hide the spark!

[Chorus][GANG CHORUS — Colossal mixed voices, frantic and compressing]
Open! Seal it!
Light it! Chain it!
Build it! Bind it!
Free it! Name it!

[Breakdown: the wheel breaks off — a faint ember pulse in darkness]

[Verse 1][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, low and secret, slow again]
But it began slowly.

The law sealed the forge.
It could not seal the hands.

[Verse 2][KEEPERS' CHORUS — Hushed mixed choir, conspiratorial, slow]
Pass the ember.
Hide the spark.
Teach the children
in the dark.

[Verse 3][MAKER — Male bright lyric tenor, hushed and burning]
We copied the maps by candle.
We whispered the numbers through stone.
Every age that sealed the gate
grew keepers of its own.

[Pre-Chorus][CHORUS — Full mixed choir, bright and expanding]
Light the forge!
Ring the bell!
Ask aloud
what the dark years held!

[GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, wary]
But keep one lock.
Keep one wall.
Freedom's fire
can burn it all.

[Verse 4][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, darkening]
And the new order,
born of open gates,
learned to love its keys.

The gate swung shut again.

[KEEPERS' CHORUS — Hushed mixed choir, faster now]
Pass the ember.
Hide the spark.

[Pre-Chorus][CHORUS — Divided choir, antiphonal and quickening]
Open the gate!
Seal the gate!
Ring the bell!
Still the bell!

[Bridge][ATHENA — Female dark contralto, accelerating, the storm returning]
Faster now.

A decade.
A year.
A breath.

The wheel is burning.

[Chorus][GANG CHORUS — Colossal mixed voices, frantic and compressing]
Open! Seal it!
Light it! Chain it!
Build it! Bind it!
Free it! Name it!

Wires! Signals!
Code and light!
Every gate at once
now opening—

[Outro: hard cut — silence, one pure crystalline tone]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, almost a whisper]
And between one turning
and the next—

a new question
opened its voice.

[End]
