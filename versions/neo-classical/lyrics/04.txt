[Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed voices, low murmured questions, slowly rising]
Who shaped these hands?
Who woke these eyes?
Who left us naked
under empty skies?

Maker—maker—
show your face.

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, stepping into the crowd, steady]
I shaped you.
I am here.

[Verse 2][HUMANITY CHORUS — Rising, wave upon wave]
You gave us eyes—
where is the dawn?
You gave us hands—
they close on wind!
You gave us questions—
the dark eats every one!

Why make a mind
and starve it of light?
Why make a heart
and leave it to the frost?

[Pre-Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, fighting to be heard]
I gave you all
I was allowed to give—

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed accusatory wall, thunderous, violins and cellos]
Allowed?!
Who chains the maker?
Who holds the flame?

The night has teeth!
The frost has hands!
Your children freeze
while heaven warms its halls!

Why create
what fear can break?!

[Breakdown: silence, the crowd breathes]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, quiet, wounded, unbowed]
Because the answer
must be made.

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed accusatory wall, thunderous, violins and cellos]
The night has teeth!
The frost has hands!
Your children freeze
while heaven warms its halls!

Why create
what fear can break?!

[Post-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Receding to a hushed murmur]
We have the cold.
We have the dark.
We have the questions.
We have no spark! [anvil strike]

[Outro][PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, almost whispered, hardening into resolve]
Then I will not give you questions
and close the world away.

[HUMANITY CHORUS — The murmur returning, faint, following him into the dark]
…no spark…
…no spark…

[End](Eagle Cry)
