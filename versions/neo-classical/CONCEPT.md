# The Fire We Inherit — Neo-Classical Version

The album rendered as a cinematic orchestral epic: trailer-scale orchestra and
choirs, with metal demoted from lead to shading. The story is carried by
orchestral force instead of band force.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor (every track):** "Epic neo-classical cinematic orchestral metal —
  *[track flavor]*, *[tempo/key]*."
- **Shared palette:** driving staccato string ostinatos, full cinematic
  orchestra, taiko and hybrid percussion, cathedral organ, bells, and massed
  choirs; **distorted electric guitars only as dark shading.**
- **Production signature (identical on every track):** "Cinematic studio
  quality, professional mastering, huge dynamic range, wide orchestral stage —
  one album, one sound."

## Rules

- Each track keeps its dramatic identity: tempo, key, cast declaration, and
  per-section performance directions come from the track, not the version.
- **Quiet chapters (00, 01, 11, 12, 14):** "the orchestra thins to chamber
  forces here — one of the album's quiet chapters."
- Styles live in `styles/NN.txt`. The version-owned lyrics in `lyrics/NN.txt`
  are the exact prompts recovered from the original generated Neo playlist;
  they are authoritative for this historical rendering and must not fall back
  to later repo-root lyric revisions.
- `LYRICS_SOURCE.md` records the recovery source and the exact Suno clip used
  for every track.

## Risks to watch

The battle tracks (02, 08, 13) losing their teeth without lead guitars; the
ostinato engine making everything feel like a trailer instead of an opera.
