Epic neo-classical cinematic orchestral metal — massed accusation scene, D minor, building from murmur to storm.
Driving staccato string ostinatos, full cinematic orchestra, taiko and hybrid percussion, cathedral organ, bells, and massed choirs; distorted electric guitars only as dark shading.
A powerful accusing chorus slowly rising against one lone bright tenor — a crowd versus a single man.
The chorus begins immediately as a circling murmur, swells in waves, and breaks into a massed accusatory wall; after the wall, a sub-drop into dead silence; the lone tenor answers, clear and unbowed.
Ending: the crowd recedes to a hushed murmur; an eagle cries; the lone voice remains, quiet and resolved.
Cinematic studio quality, professional mastering, huge dynamic range, wide orchestral stage — one album, one sound.
