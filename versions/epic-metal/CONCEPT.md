# The Fire We Inherit — Epic Metal Version

The album rendered as one unified heavy symphonic power-metal opera. This is the
"shared stage" version: every track stands on the same instrumentation so the
fifteen tracks read as one continuous work.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor (every track):** "Epic dark symphonic power metal opera — *[track flavor]*, *[tempo/key]*."
- **Shared palette:** riff-driven distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless
  double-kick, war drums, cathedral organ, low brass, strings, bells, and massed
  choirs — "the album's shared stage."
- **Production signature (identical on every track):** "Epic studio quality,
  professional mastering, massive low end, punchy drums, wide choirs — one
  album, one sound."

## Rules

- Each track keeps its dramatic identity: tempo, key, cast declaration, and
  per-section performance directions come from the track, not the version.
- **Quiet chapters (00, 01, 11, 12, 14):** carry the restraint line — "the
  shared metal stage falls almost silent here … its weight implied, not
  played." The metal identity is preserved through what is withheld.
- Styles live in `styles/NN.txt`; lyrics always come from the repo-root
  `songs/` directory (single source of truth across versions).

## Risks to watch

The quiet chapters flattening under the shared stage; the awakening blooms
(03, 05) reading as generic power-metal triumph instead of earned wonder.
