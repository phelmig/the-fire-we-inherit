Epic dark symphonic power metal opera — spoken overture, 60 BPM, D minor.
Riff-driven distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, war drums, cathedral organ, low brass, strings, bells, and massed choirs — the album's shared stage.
Restraint: the shared metal stage falls almost silent here — one of the album's quiet chapters, its weight implied, not played.
Choir: wordless ethereal female singing, swelling gradually beneath the voice.
Narration: dry, intimate, commanding — spoken, unhurried.
Duel: the organ argues for control, one ascending violin for creation — trading phrases beneath the question, intertwining unresolved over the crystalline pulse, gathering into the finale.
Finale: massed voices declaim once, then thunder.
Ending: unresolved beneath rising distant thunder.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, massive low end, punchy drums, wide choirs — one album, one sound.
