[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving and commanding]
Son of the king who devoured his own,
hidden and sworn to return.
Zeus — back for the stolen, the swallowed, the throne,
teaching the tyrant to burn!

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving]
Out of the Titans a seer stepped forth,
choosing the side of the dawn:
"I am Prometheus — I've seen every road.
Stand with the future or fall!"

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, shouted]
Ten years! Hold the line!
Fire and stone! Thrones on the pyre!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Into the war of the titans we ride!

Freeing the fallen and breaking the chains —
the age of the swallowing ends in the flames!

[Verse 3][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, urgent]

Kronos will fall by the morn!

must now answer why.
