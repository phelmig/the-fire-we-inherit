[Intro: one bell. Wind. A hammer falls once — then the march ignites]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, grim and driving]
Out of the vessel came sorrow,
and out of the sorrow came law.
Fear found a voice and a language:
"Remember the price that you saw!"

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
A lock on the grain and a mark on the door,
a guard at the gates of the mind.
The Guardian swore on the graves of the lost:
"No open hand left behind!"

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, rigid chant]
Lock the grain! Mark the door!
License the question! Count the store!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, vast and cold]
Sealing the forges and silencing songs,
the law of the fearful is marching along!
Counting the questions and guarding the grain —
the age of the making is bound in a chain!

[Verse 3][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, dark and measured]
So the tale of the trap became order,
the warning was carved into stone.
The fire that woke them was rationed and caged —
and the forge fell as cold as the throne!

[Bridge][MAKER — Male young soaring power metal tenor, one cry against the choir]
The vessel was not our crime!

[GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, crushing]
Remember the vessel! Remember the cry!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, rigid chant]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, vast and cold]

[The machinery slows. Wind returns.]

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet and spoken]
The forge fell silent, the hammers hung,
the songs of the making no longer sung.
But under the ash where no law looked,
one ember kept its breath.

[End](Wind through a cold forge. One faint ember pulse, like a heartbeat.)
