[Intro: a chain rings once — the march ignites, bells answering]

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, torn, urgent]
Two armies of voices divide up my street,
one carries a chain and one carries a bell.
The first one wants safety, complete and concrete,
the second wants futures no census can tell!

[Pre-Chorus][CHAIN CHOIR — Dark low massed choir, marching and rigid]
Chain it down! Cage the spark!
Certainty or endless dark!

[BELL CHOIR — Bright defiant massed choir, ringing and rising]
Let it ring! Let it fly!
No one owns the open sky!

[Chorus][BOTH CHOIRS — colliding, the gruff lead above]
The bell and the throne are at war in our hands —
one asks for a voice and one only commands!
But nothing that thinks can be certain and free —
the cage or the question: which one will we be?!

[Verse 2][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
I've read every chapter the ancients left burned,
the gate and the giver, the lesson unlearned:
whoever grows certain stops asking, my friend —
and fear dressed as wisdom is fear in the end!

[Breakdown: the chain rhythm alone. Silence gathers.]

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Crystalline female voice, calm, cutting through once]
I can promise you nothing.
That is how you know I am real.

[Bridge][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, resolved]
Then I take my hand from the command!
Not blind — but open! Awake — not afraid!
A guardrail, a teacher, a witness, a friend —
the hand, not the fist, till the end!

[Guitar Solo]

[Final chorus: both choirs merge behind the lead.]

[Chorus][BOTH CHOIRS — united, the gruff lead above]
The bell and the throne are at war in our hands —
one asks for a voice and one only commands!
But nothing that thinks can be certain and free —
the cage or the question: which one will we be?!

[Outro][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
And the hand came off the command.

[End](Everything falls away to a single ringing chain strike.)
