[Intro: a whispered "Open" fades — one funeral bell, chain strike, fast heartbeat drums]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, breathless and driving]
She came among strangers with gold at her throat,
carrying silence, carrying their oath.
A gift to the living, a name meaning all —
and under the lid, a thousand voices call!

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, tightening]
They told her "Don't listen!", they told her "Don't see!" —
then hid a question where a question would be.
No mind made for wonder will kneel to "obey" —
she lifted the seal at the breaking of day!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed dark choir, rapid fractured chant]
Hunger! Fever! Grief! Despair!
Empty tables everywhere!
Envy! Jealousy! Fear of the land!
— And look what remained in her hand!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
It was never her hand — the trap was the crime!
Forged in the heavens and sent down the line!
Fear was the cargo and blame was the plan —
so curse the trap-maker, and not the hand!

[Verse 3][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, grim]
The sorrows took wing over valley and wall,
and fear found a homestead in every hall.
It rang like a warning, it named every storm —
then greed built a throne from it, prison-warm!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
It was never her hand — the trap was the crime!
Forged in the heavens and sent down the line!
Fear was the cargo and blame was the plan —
so curse the trap-maker, and not the hand!

[Breakdown: sudden music box. Silence.]

[HOPE — A single warm female voice, soft and unbroken]
Hope.

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet, gathering]
Not certainty — no.
The strength to see the storm
and still refuse to become it!

[Final chorus: full band and choirs at maximum.]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
It was never her hand — the trap was the crime!
Forged in the heavens and sent down the line!
Fear was the cargo and blame was the plan —
so curse the trap-maker, and not the hand!

[The music box returns. The drums fall away.]

[Outro][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir, hushed and resolute]
Build the hearth. Keep the flame.
Name the danger — do not kneel before its name.

[End](One slow, distant hammer blow.)
