[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving]
They forged it in secret and wrapped it in gold,
a beautiful promise designed to unfold.
They knew that a wonder can't help but to see —
the trap only works if the curious are free!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed dark choir, rapid fractured chant]
Hunger! Fever! Grief! Despair!
Empty tables everywhere!
Envy! Spite! Fear of the land!
— And look for a hand! Always a hand!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
Don't blame the hand — blame the ones who designed it!
Fear was the cargo and heaven consigned it!
The bell of the warning may ring through the land —
but curse the trap-maker, and never the hand!
