[Intro: the valley's murmur still hangs on the cold wind][HUMANITY CHORUS — Dark distant murmur, fading]
…no spark… no spark…

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving and resolute]
Born out of clay, left in the cold,
young minds awake in the night.
Heaven has fire it refuses to share,
guarding the flame on the height!

[Verse 2][PROMETHEUS — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
I made a promise the day they awoke:
never abandoned, never alone!
The law of the thunder commands me to wait —
I answer to them, not the throne!

[Pre-Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising]
I know what it costs! I go alone!
One ember hidden — carried down!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Out of the darkness and into the light,
carrying fire through the merciless night!
Breaking the law of the king of the sky —
the promise is kept and the flame will not die!

[Breakdown: one dry fennel stalk catches fire. The dark chant stirs.]

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, breaking out of the dark]
Warmth!

[HUMANITY CHOIR — The dark voices igniting into warm radiant song]
Light! Bread! Iron! Home!
The night has teeth — but so does the flame!
The frost has hands — and now so do we!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Out of the darkness and into the light,
carrying fire through the merciless night!
Breaking the law of the king of the sky —
the promise is kept and the flame will not die!

[Guitar Solo]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male gruff powerful baritone, defiant]
If they cannot choose their fate,
why were they born at all?
No hand was made for chains!
No mind was made to kneel!

[Final chorus: full band, keyboard-brass and choirs at maximum.]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Out of the darkness and into the light,
carrying fire through the merciless night!
Breaking the law of the king of the sky —
the promise is kept and the flame will not die!

[The music cuts to a low synth pad and distant thunder.]
