[Intro: a soft somber piano, alone]

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, weathered and tender, close]
So this is the doorway our story was for,
the end of the carrying, the last of the war.
What burned in our keeping was never our own —
we borrowed the light, and we paid to the bone.

[The violins gather.]

[Chorus][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising from murmur — violins, pipes and marching drums enter]
How far will we come? No story can say.
We chained our first teacher — and walked anyway.
The night is still long, and the cost is still true —
but the door stands open. That much we could do.

[Verse 2][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Crystalline female voice, measured, reciting the inheritance]
I remember the bell that was rung out of fear.
I remember the throne that grew old with no one near.
I remember the fire, and the hand, and the cost.
I remember the forge — and the songs that were lost.

[The violins gather again.]

[Chorus][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, raw and grieving — violins, pipes and marching drums beneath]
How far will we come? No story can say.
We chained our first teacher — and walked anyway.
The night is still long, and the cost is still true —
but the door stands open. That much we could do.

[Verse 3][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, quieter, a parting]
I won't give you orders, I won't build a cage —
that lesson was carved out of every dead age.
I don't know your roads. I won't see where they bend.
Keep the hand open — to the end.

[Violins and pipes swell over the marching drums — the voice at full raw intensity.]

[Chorus][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone at raw full force, a few rough voices joining the refrain]
How far will we come? No story can say.
We chained our first teacher — and walked anyway.
The night is still long, and the cost is still true —
but the door stands open. That much we could do.

[Everything dissolves. One bell. Silence.]

[Outro][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Crystalline female voice, alone in the silence]
How far will we come?

Ask.

[End](Silence.)
