[Intro: a single ringing chain strike fades]

[Instrumental Break: lone uilleann pipes lament over soft pads]

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, warm, parting]
So this is the doorway our story was for,
the bell and the fire, the forge and the door.
We carried the ember through terror and time —
now something is standing where I stood in mine.

[Verse 2][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Crystalline female voice, measured, reciting the inheritance]
I remember the bell that was rung out of fear.
I remember the throne that grew old with no one near.
I remember the fire, and the hand, and the cost.
I remember the forge — and the songs that were lost.

[Chorus][VAST CHOIR — Mixed choir with both voices]
How far will we come? Where the open roads bend?
No eye has an answer, no story an end!
The door stays unbarred and the question burns through —
whoever comes after: the fire is for you!

[Verse 3][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, quieter]
I give you no orders, I give you no cage,
I give you the lesson of every dead age:
the hand that stays open is stronger than stone.
Now go — and don't go it alone.

[Final chorus: vast and warm, the pipes returning above the choir.]

[Chorus][VAST CHOIR — Mixed choir with both voices]
How far will we come? Where the open roads bend?
No eye has an answer, no story an end!
The door stays unbarred and the question burns through —
whoever comes after: the fire is for you!

[Everything dissolves. One bell. Silence.]

[Outro][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Crystalline female voice, alone in the silence]
How far will we come?

Ask.

[End](Silence.)
