[Intro: a low drone. A distant military snare approaches out of darkness]

[Intro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, spoken, dry and commanding, begins immediately]
Humanity.
How far have we come?

[The war drums swell. A wordless low choir hum rises]

[NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, spoken, measured]
Every wall we raised,
every fire we carried —
and still we stand divided
over the question we inherited:

Must control come before creation?
Or does creation begin
where control must end?

[NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, gathering force]
To seek the answer, we go back —
past every fire we ever lit —
to the myths of ancient Greece.
To the one who lit the first:

Prometheus.
The teacher who gave humanity fire —
and suffered for its freedom.

[MASSED CHOIR — One thunderous declamation]
THIS IS HIS STORY!

[End](Thunder answers.)
