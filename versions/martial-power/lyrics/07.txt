[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, erupting from stillness in wounded fury]
I chained the thief upon the mountain —
and still their fires burn!
A thousand hearths below my sky
laugh upward at my law!
I broke the devouring throne!
I shielded this whole age!
And clay — clay looks into my heaven
and does not kneel!

[Pre-Chorus][OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massed dark choir, fearful obedient chant]
His grief is law! His fear is command!
No god will hold the thunder's hand!

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with the full dark choir]
Forge them a gift out of beauty and clay,
perfect and shining, to take breath away!
Seal every sorrow beneath its bright lid —
let them discover what heaven hid!

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, cold procedure, each word placed like stone]
Punishment has marked the crime —
it has not answered the firelight.
So bring me the forge. Fashion a woman:
all gifts gathered in one name.
She is not the weapon — hear me —
she is the dawn they will not doubt.
The vessel rides beside her,
and fear waits inside.

[Bridge][OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massed dark choir, ritual forging chant over anvils and harp]
One gave beauty! One gave breath!
One gave language! One hid death!
No single hand would bear the blame —
so all of heaven forged the shame!
