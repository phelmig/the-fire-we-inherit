I chained the thief upon the mountain —
and still their fires burn!
A thousand hearths below my sky
laugh upward at my law!
I broke the devouring throne!
I shielded this whole age!
And clay — clay looks into my heaven
and does not kneel!

[Pre-Chorus][OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massed dark choir, fearful obedient chant]
His grief is law! His fear is command!
No god will hold the thunder's hand!

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with the full dark choir]
Forge them a gift out of beauty and clay,
perfect and shining, to take breath away!
Seal every sorrow beneath its bright lid —
let them discover what heaven hid!

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, cold and deliberate]
You cannot unburn what the fire has taught,
you cannot unfree what freedom has wrought!

a gift for the living, a trap built to shine!
So bring me the forge! Shape a dawn out of clay,
so perfect no mortal will turn her away!
Call her Pandora — all gifts in one name —
and sealed at her side rides the vessel of pain!
