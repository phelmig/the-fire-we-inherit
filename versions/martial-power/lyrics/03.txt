[Intro: wet clay turns inside an open hand — bells toll, organ and march drums gather]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, proclaiming over the march]
The thunder took the heavens and the elder crown was down,
the sky was set in order and the law was handed down.
But the Foreseer at the river saw the ages yet to run —
every day the same tomorrow, every dawn a finished sun.

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, gathering force]
He had seen the endless endings and he named them all the same:
not a wonder, not a question — only quarrels for a name.
So he knelt beside the water and he took the living clay,
and the host of heaven wondered as he worked till break of day.

[Pre-Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, rising hymn]
From the river clay — the hands! For the dawning — eyes!
A mind that no one owns shall rise! Shall rise!

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed triumphant male choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[Post-Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, ringing]
Glory to the making! Glory to the dawn!

[Breakdown: the march halts. One heartbeat. The organ holds one low chord.]

[MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, hushed and awed]
It wakes… it wakes…

[NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
And the first word
out of the clay
was "Why?"

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, breaking into wonder]
And the one who saw all endings stood as blinded by the light,
for the road beyond that question ran past every gift of sight.
And he laughed as chains were falling: "It is free of prophecy!
At last — a new tomorrow that no eye will ever see!"

[The march returns — organ and bells thundering, greater than before.]

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed triumphant male choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[Instrumental Break: cathedral organ and bells thunder over the march]

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed triumphant male choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[Post-Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, ringing]
Glory to the making! Glory to the dawn!

[The light dims. Cold strings gather.]

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The world was finished.
Now it was begun.

[End](First night falls on the newly awake. Far off, voices begin to murmur.)
