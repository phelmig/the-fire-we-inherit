[Intro: wet clay turns inside an open hand — bells toll, organ and march drums gather]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, proclaiming over the march]
When the war had left the heavens and the world lay dressed in law,
by the river knelt a Titan with tomorrow in his awe.
It was Prometheus the Foreseer, and he shaped us out of clay —
so that something past all foresight would awaken on that day.

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, gathering force]
And we rose out of the river with the morning on our skin,
with the hunger of the asking and a fire-want within.
We were given hands for building and the eyes to see the far —
we were given every question, but not a single star.

[Pre-Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, rising hymn]
From the clay — to the breath! From the breath — to the cry!
From the cry — to the question: why?

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir with the gruff lead on top]
From the clay we rose to wonder, from the wonder to the "why" —
we were made to ask the heavens, we were born to search the sky!
But the fire stayed above us and the night came crawling in:
we were given all the questions — and no light to see them in!

[Breakdown: the march halts. One heartbeat. The organ holds one low chord.]

[MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, hushed and awed]
The sun goes down… the first night falls…

[NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
And in the dark, ten thousand voices
asked the question for the first time:
"Why?"

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising]
And the one who gave the question stood in silence at the sight:
he had made a mind for morning — and the world was made of night.
What is waking without seeing? What is hunger without bread?
He had given us the dawn-thirst — but the light stayed overhead.

[The march returns — organ and bells thundering, darker and greater.]

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir with the gruff lead on top]
From the clay we rose to wonder, from the wonder to the "why" —
we were made to ask the heavens, we were born to search the sky!
But the fire stayed above us and the night came crawling in:
we were given all the questions — and no light to see them in!

[Instrumental Break: cathedral organ and bells thunder over the march]

[Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir with the gruff lead on top]
From the clay we rose to wonder, from the wonder to the "why" —
we were made to ask the heavens, we were born to search the sky!
But the fire stayed above us and the night came crawling in:
we were given all the questions — and no light to see them in!

[Post-Chorus][MALE CHOIR — Massed male choir, ringing, fading]
From the clay — to the breath! From the breath — to the cry!
From the cry — to the question: why?

[The light dims. Cold strings gather.]

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The world was finished.
Now it was begun.

[End](First night falls on the newly awake. Far off, voices begin to murmur.)
