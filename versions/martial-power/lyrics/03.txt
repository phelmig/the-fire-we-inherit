[Intro: wet clay turns inside an open hand — water over stone, a soft heartbeat drum]

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, measured, first person]
They call me the Foreseer — I've read every page,
each ending the same, an unturnable age.
The rivers won't wonder, the mountains won't ask,
and gods only argue whose name's on the past!

[Pre-Chorus][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising]
But down by the river the clay's lying still —
unwritten, unspoken, unbent to a will!
From clay — the hands! For dawn — the eyes!
A mind that no one owns shall rise!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
I will make what I cannot command,
a fate that will slip from my hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[The bloom: the music opens into radiant warmth — bells and full choir]

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, breathy and newly awake]
Light… moving through the leaves.
Sound… inside of me.

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, one clear cry]
Why?

[Bridge][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, triumphant wonder]
I do not know what you will become!
I've seen every ending — but this one has none!
At last — a tomorrow no eye has read!
The world isn't finished — it's beginning instead!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Warm radiant massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
I will make what I cannot command,
a fate that will slip from my hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[Guitar Solo]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Warm radiant massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
I will make what I cannot command,
a fate that will slip from my hand!

[The light dims. Cold strings gather.]

[Outro][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The world was finished.
Now it was begun.

[End](First night falls on the newly awake. Far off, voices begin to murmur.)
