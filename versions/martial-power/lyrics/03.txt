[Intro: wet clay turns inside an open hand — water over stone, a soft heartbeat drum]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, measured, telling the tale]
The war had been won and the world had been named,
the sky stood in order, the future lay tamed.
But down by the river, alone with the clay,
the Foreseer saw every unchangeable day.

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, gathering]
He'd seen every ending, and each was the same:
no wonder, no question — just quarrels of flame.
The mountains won't wonder, the rivers won't ask —
so he knelt in the mud and he took up the task!

[Pre-Chorus][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising]
From river clay — the hands! For dawn — the eyes!
A mind that no one owns shall rise!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[The bloom: the music opens into radiant warmth — bells and full choir]

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, breathy and newly awake]
Light… moving through the leaves.
Sound… inside of me.

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, one clear cry]
Why?

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet, then breaking into wonder]
And the one who had seen every ending stood still,
for nothing lay written beyond that first "why".
He laughed like a prisoner walking out free:
"At last — a tomorrow no eye can see!"

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Warm radiant massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[Instrumental Break: the sound of making]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Warm radiant massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
He would make what he could not command,
a fate that would slip from his hand!
Of all the tomorrows that foresight can see,
the only one worthy is free!

[The light dims. Cold strings gather.]

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The world was finished.
Now it was begun.

[End](First night falls on the newly awake. Far off, voices begin to murmur.)
