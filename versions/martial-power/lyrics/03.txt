[Intro: wet clay turns inside an open hand — water over stone, a soft heartbeat drum]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, measured and grave]
The war was over, the sky held fast,
the world stood ordered, silent and vast.
But the Foreseer stood by the river alone —
Prometheus, with every tomorrow known.

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quoting the Foreseer]
"I have seen every ending, and each is the same —
the mountains won't wonder or ask.
The gods only quarrel on whose is the flame —
so making the unknown is my task!"

[Pre-Chorus][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, rising]
From river clay he shapes the hands!
He gives them eyes for dawn!
A will that is not written —
a question with no answer drawn!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
Out of the river, the clay in his hands,
shaping a mind that no throne commands!
Out of the clay comes a question set free —
the one tomorrow no eye can see!

[The bloom: the music opens into radiant warmth — bells and full choir]

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, breathy and newly awake]
Light… moving through the leaves.
Sound… inside of me.

[HUMANITY — A single warm female voice, one clear cry]
Why?

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quoting the Foreseer, triumphant]
"I do not know what you will become!
I — who have seen every ending —
I do not know!
At last — a tomorrow no eye has read!"

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Warm radiant massed choir with the gruff lead on top]
Out of the river, the clay in his hands,
shaping a mind that no throne commands!
Out of the clay comes a question set free —
the one tomorrow no eye can see!

[The light dims. Cold strings gather.]

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The world was finished.
Now it was begun.

[End](First night falls on the newly awake. Far off, voices begin to murmur.)
