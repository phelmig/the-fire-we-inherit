[Intro: thunder extinguishes every sound except the mortal hearth]

[Intro][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, screaming across the sky in raw fury]
PROMETHEUS!

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving and grim]
When conscience and law drew their lines in the sky,
he stood at the border and would not deny:
"I did what was right and I'll answer alone —
you may break this body, the fire has flown!"

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, shouted]
Sentenced to suffer! Never to die!
Bound to the rock at the roof of the sky!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Chained to the mountain, condemned to the sky,
the eagle descending as ages go by!
But down in the valley the fires remain —
you never can capture the gift with the chain!

[Verse 2][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
The verdict was iron, eternal the cost,
a sentence designed so that nothing is lost:
the flesh that renews for the talons that came —
but pain has no power to unlight a flame!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, shouted]
Sentenced to suffer! Never to die!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]

[Bridge][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, solemn over half-time crush]
Strike! The flesh renews forever —
so does the fire he set free!
Every dawn the eagle rises —
every dawn a thousand hearths burn on!
