[Intro: thunder extinguishes every sound except the mortal hearth]

[Intro][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, screaming across the sky in raw fury]
PROMETHEUS!

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, driving and grim]
Summoned to answer the theft of the flame,
he stood before thunder alone:
"You call it a crime — I call it my word.
I answer for what I have done!"

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
"Who gave you the right to choose for them all?"
the king of the heavens replied.
"And who gave you yours to make sure they could not?" —
no thunder could turn him aside!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir, shouted]
Sentenced to suffer! Never to die!
Bound to the rock at the roof of the sky!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed male gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
Chained to the mountain, condemned to the sky,
the eagle descending as ages go by!
But down in the valley the fires remain —
you never can capture the gift with the chain!

[Bridge][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, solemn over half-time crush]
Strike! His flesh renews forever —
so does the fire he set free!
Every dawn the eagle rises —
every dawn another hearth burns on!

[Chain strikes lock into the mountain. Wings cut through rain.]
