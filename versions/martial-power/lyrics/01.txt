[Intro: the thunder from the prologue dissolves into a primordial drone — deep war drums begin]

[Verse 1][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, near-spoken and grave]
In the beginning was Chaos —
no sky above, no law below.
Then earth took form, then heaven rose,
and the first king feared what the earth could grow.

[THRONE CHOIR — Massed dark male choir, slow ritual chant]
Close the gate! Seal the dawn!
Bury the future before it is born!

[Verse 2][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, harder]
But what is buried learns the walls.
Kronos broke the elder chain —
then took the crown and named it peace,
and heard one son would end his reign.

The gate swung shut. The feast began.
He swallowed each tomorrow before tomorrow ran.

[THRONE CHOIR — Massed dark male choir, heavier, devouring]
Close the gate! Seal the age!
Keep every heir inside the cage!

[Breakdown: one heartbeat beneath mountain wind]

[Verse 3][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, low and intimate]
But one child vanished from the hunger of the throne.
No herald spoke. No court was told.
Only mountain dark, a heartbeat,
and shields against the stone.

[HIDDEN CHOIR — Hushed voices behind shield strikes]
Hide the breath. Drown the cry.
Let the nameless child survive.

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, almost whispered]
His name was Zeus.
He learned the world behind a closed gate.
He learned what fear will make of fate.

In the beginning was Chaos.
Then power learned to fear what came next.

[End](Shield strikes conceal a heartbeat. Distant thunder draws near.)
