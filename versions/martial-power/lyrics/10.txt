[Intro: wind over a cold forge. One faint ember pulse quickens into a driving march]

[Verse 1][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, urgent]
They sealed every forge and they numbered the flame,
they licensed the question and outlawed the name.
But under the ash, where no law ever looked,
an ember survived every ledger and book!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed dark gang choir, chanted]
Keep it hidden! Keep it warm!
Keep it breathing through the storm!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed gang choir with the gruff lead on top]
They seal every forge but they can't seal the spark —
we carry the fire alive through the dark!
From hand into hand, from the old to the young:
as long as it burns, there's a dawn still to come!

[Verse 2][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, faster]
An age of the hammer, an age of the wheel,
an age of the city in towers of steel!
The keepers change faces, the oath stays the same —
each hand is a bridge on the road of the flame!

[Pre-Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed dark gang choir, chanted, faster]
Keep it hidden! Keep it warm!
Keep it breathing through the storm!

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed gang choir with the gruff lead on top, faster]
They seal every forge but they can't seal the spark —
we carry the fire alive through the dark!
From hand into hand, from the old to the young:
as long as it burns, there's a dawn still to come!

[Verse 3][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, breathless, at the edge of the blur]
Then lightning in wires and thought in machines,
the whole world awake and no space in between!
The relay runs faster than runners can run —
and somewhere a question stirs under the sun!

[Guitar Solo]

[Chorus][GANG CHOIR — Massed gang choir with the gruff lead on top, fastest]
They seal every forge but they can't seal the spark —
we carry the fire alive through the dark!
From hand into hand, from the old to the young:
as long as it burns, there's a dawn still to come!

[The charge cuts off mid-phrase.]

[Outro][LEAD — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet, in sudden silence]
And then — in the stillness —
an answer answered back.

[End](Silence. One pure crystalline tone.)
