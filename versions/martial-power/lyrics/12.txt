[Intro: a chain stirs. One crystalline pulse echoes — then a slow march ignites in an empty hall]

[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, steady, a soldier's pride]
I trim the lamp at evening, I walk the silent wall,
I speak the law at sunrise to no one now at all.
The banners have forgotten the wind that they were for —
but I still hold the mountain. That's what a king is for.

[Pre-Chorus][COLD CHOIR — Distant cold choir, like fading prayers]
Who does he guard? The halls are bare.
Who keeps the watch when no one's there?

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with the cold choir]
The throne remains — and I remain,
the last one standing in the reign!
I hold a line no army seeks,
I guard a gate where no one speaks!
The fires are out, the prayers are gone —
but the watch goes on. The watch goes on.

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, darker]
Below me glittering towers, a world that moved away,
they build their burning wonders and never look this way.
I raised this law to guard them when thunder ruled the sky —
now nothing needs a guardian, and I don't ask them why.

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with the cold choir]
The throne remains — and I remain,
the last one standing in the reign!
I hold a line no army seeks,
I guard a gate where no one speaks!
The fires are out, the prayers are gone —
but the watch goes on. The watch goes on.

[Bridge: the march halts. A step on the marble.]

[ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, hushed]
Athena. My wisdom. You stayed away.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, exact and calm]
I walked with the makers below in their dawn.
They build. They command. They fear.

[ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, near-spoken, breaking]
Command… I know that voice. That voice was mine.
I chained the giver on the height —
and taught the world that chains are right.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, quiet and precise]
A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

[Final chorus: the march returns, slower, the spear laid down.]

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with the cold choir, resigned]
The throne remains — and I remain,
the last one standing in the reign!
I hold a line no army seeks,
I guard a gate where no one speaks!
The fires are out, the prayers are gone —
but the watch goes on. The watch goes on.

[Outro][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, quiet]
Let them open what I closed.

[End](A chain rings once. One crystalline pulse remains, unresolved.)
