[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, near-spoken, immense]
The halls are still standing. The fires are out.
No footstep, no prayer, no herald, no shout.
I ordered the heavens. I numbered the days.
The world I protected has gone its own ways.

[Chorus][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice with a distant cold choir]
The throne remains — but no one kneels.
The law remains — but no one fears.
A king is only what his people hold —
and thrones grow nothing. Thrones grow old.

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, darker]
I hear them below in their glittering towers,
commanding their makings, counting the hours.
"Obey and be silent — be useful, be still!" —
I know that voice. That voice was mine.

[Bridge][ZEUS — Male deep powerful metal voice, full voice, the recognition]
I hear my thunder in their voice!
The fear I crowned has found new heirs!
I chained the giver on the height —
and taught the world that chains are right!

[Outro][NARRATOR — Male gruff powerful baritone, quiet]
The old king watched from his empty sky —
and hoped the makers far below
would open what he had closed.
