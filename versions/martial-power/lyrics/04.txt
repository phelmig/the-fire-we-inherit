[Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, low murmured questions, slowly rising]
Who shaped these hands?
Who woke these eyes?
Who left us naked
under empty skies?

[Verse 1][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, rising wave upon wave]
You gave us eyes — where is the dawn?
You gave us hands — they close on wind!
You gave us questions — every one
is eaten by the dark again!

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, chanted]
Maker! Maker! Show your face!
Maker! Maker! Show your face!

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, thunderous accusing wall]
The night has teeth and the frost has hands!
Your children freeze while heaven stands!
You gave us questions, you gave us sight —
so why did you starve us of light?!

[Verse 2][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, harder, colder]
We have the cold, we have the dark,
we have the hunger and the fear!
We have a thousand questions burning —
and not one spark to see them clear!

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, chanted]
Maker! Maker! Show your face!
Maker! Maker! Show your face!

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, thunderous accusing wall]
The night has teeth and the frost has hands!
Your children freeze while heaven stands!
You gave us questions, you gave us sight —
so why did you starve us of light?!

[Breakdown: sub-drop into dead silence. The crowd breathes]

[PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, quiet, wounded, unbowed]
I hear you.
And the answer must be made.

[Final chorus: the crowd at full storm.]

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed dark choir, thunderous accusing wall]
The night has teeth and the frost has hands!
Your children freeze while heaven stands!
You gave us questions, you gave us sight —
so why did you starve us of light?!

[Post-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Receding to a hushed murmur, following him into the dark]
…no spark…
…no spark…

[End](The murmur fades on the cold wind. An eagle cries far off.)
