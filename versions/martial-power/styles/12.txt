Martial doom-march elegy, vast and desolate, 60 BPM, E minor.
Crushing slow distorted electric rhythm guitars, funeral war drums, deep bells, low synth-brass, cold pads, sparse piano.
Voices: one deep powerful male metal voice as the old king, near-spoken verses hardening into full voice; one gruff male narrator line at the end; a distant cold choir like fading prayers; no other singers.
Verses: slow, immense, every chord a monument.
Chorus: monumental and restrained, no triumph, grief in granite.
Ending: a chain sound and one unresolved crystalline pulse.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, massive slow low end, cavernous space, wide cold choirs.
