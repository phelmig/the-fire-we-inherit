Heroic heavy metal march anthem with a broken heart, solemn last-stand energy, 112 BPM, E minor.
Driving distorted electric rhythm guitars, steady martial snare, deep bells, dark synth-brass wall, pounding electric bass.
Voices: one deep powerful male metal voice as the old king, proud and weathered, carrying the song; one icy exact female contralto arriving only at the bridge, cold and precise; a distant cold choir answering; no other singers.
Verses: steady soldier's march, dignified, driving.
Chorus: big solemn singalong, heroic and hollow, bells tolling.
Bridge: the march halts — hushed exchange over bare marble silence, then the march returns slower, resigned.
Ending: a chain rings once, one unresolved crystalline pulse.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy modern metal mix, wide cold choirs.
