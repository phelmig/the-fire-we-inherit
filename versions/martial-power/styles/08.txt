Martial heavy power metal gallop, breathless and defiant, 160 BPM, B minor.
Fast galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, urgent toms, martial snare, massive synth-brass wall, pounding electric bass, funeral bells, and lone mournful uilleann pipes reserved for the breakdown and the final chorus.
Voices: one gruff powerful male baritone lead carrying the song; a massed dark choir in rapid fractured chant; one warm female voice with a single word in the breakdown; no other singers.
Verses: driving and hunted, tight riffs under the lead.
Chorus: huge galloping singalong hook, gang choir with the lead on top, mourning and defiant.
Breakdown: sudden music box, then the uilleann pipes lament over it, fragile, then the single warm word.
Final chorus: the pipes ride high above the full wall.
Ending: the music box returns; one distant hammer blow.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy modern metal mix, loud guitars, massive choirs.
