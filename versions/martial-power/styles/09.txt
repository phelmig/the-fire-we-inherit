Martial heavy power metal march, grim war-machine energy, 112 BPM, E minor.
Chugging distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, martial snare, massive synth-brass and keyboard wall, pounding electric bass, anvil hits.
Opening: one bell, wind, a single hammer blow — then the march ignites.
Voices: one gruff powerful male baritone lead carrying the chronicle; one young soaring tenor cry near the end only; massed male gang choir in rigid chant; no other singers.
Verses: relentless mid-tempo march, tight riffs under the lead.
Chorus: massive chanted war hymn with the keyboard-brass wall — vast and oppressive, never triumphant.
Solo: melodic overdriven electric lead guitar over the march.
Ending: sudden emptiness — wind over a cold forge, one faint ember pulse.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy modern metal mix, thick keyboard-brass wall, loud guitars, massive gang choirs.
