Somber folk lament, bare and unpolished, slow 6/8, D minor.
A soft somber piano runs continuously through the whole song — the only instrument in the verses.
Violins enter alone for the build-ups; violins and Irish pipes together carry the powerful parts; marching snare drums only beneath the powerful parts. No other instruments.
Voices: one gruff powerful male baritone lead, weathered, close and dry, telling the story through vocal intensity alone — from murmur to raw grief and back; one crystalline female voice, pure and calm, for the remembrance verse and the final word; a few rough voices joining only the last refrain; no other singers.
Verses: voice and the soft piano alone, unhurried, human; the first voice begins almost immediately.
Refrains: violins, pipes and marching drums — the voice rises raw, sung with force, not polish.
Ending: everything falls away to one bell; a single quiet word alone in silence.
Intimate live-room quality, close dry vocals, minimal production.
