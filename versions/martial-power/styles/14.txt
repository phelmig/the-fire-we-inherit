Somber Celtic folk-metal lament, graveside waltz feel, slow 6/8, D minor with one warm major turn.
Lone uilleann pipes and low whistle over sparse acoustic guitar and soft piano; a deep bass-drum pulse; the full heavy band — crushing slow distorted electric rhythm guitars, war drums — swelling only for the final chorus, then falling away.
Voices: one gruff powerful male baritone lead, weathered and tender, close to the ear; one crystalline female voice, pure and calm, for the remembrance verse and the final word; a low mourning choir humming beneath the later choruses; no other singers.
Verses: hushed, unhurried, every line a farewell.
Chorus: a slow rising lament, sung not shouted, heavier each return.
Ending: everything dissolves to one bell; a single quiet word alone in silence.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, warm acoustic detail, deep low end in the swell, vast quiet ending.
