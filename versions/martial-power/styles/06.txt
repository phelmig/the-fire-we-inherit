Martial heavy power metal war anthem, 132 BPM, B minor.

Chorus: huge singalong war hymn, gang choir with the lead on top, keyboards blazing.
Bridge: half-time crush over chains and anvils, then double-time charge.
Solo: melodic overdriven electric lead guitar.
Ending: three forge blows, one unresolved bell.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy modern metal mix, thick keyboard-brass wall, loud guitars, massive gang choirs.
