Martial heavy power metal accusation anthem, a massed crowd as the lead voice, building from murmur to storm, 120 BPM, D minor.
Chugging distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, martial snare, massive synth-brass and keyboard wall, pounding electric bass.
Voices: a massed dark choir carries the whole song as the lead — a freezing crowd, wave upon wave; one lone bright tenor line in the breakdown only; no other singers.
Opens as a circling murmur that swells in marching waves into a chanted wall.
Chorus: the crowd at full storm, thunderous and accusing, instantly singable.
Breakdown: sub-drop into dead silence before the lone voice answers.
Ending: the crowd recedes to a hushed murmur; an eagle cries far off.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy modern metal mix, thick keyboard-brass wall, loud guitars, colossal crowd choirs.
