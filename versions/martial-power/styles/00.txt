Martial spoken prologue, solemn processional, 60 BPM, D minor.
A low drone, distant military snare, deep war drums swelling slowly, low synth-brass pads, one distant bell; a wordless low male choir hum rising beneath the voice.
Voices: one gruff powerful male narrator, spoken, dry and commanding, begins immediately; a wordless low male choir that declaims once at the end; no other singers.
The march builds from silence to a vast rolling processional, never fast, never bright.
Finale: massed voices declaim once, then thunder.
Ending: unresolved beneath rising distant thunder.
