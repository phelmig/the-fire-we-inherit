# The Fire We Inherit — Epic Metal Codex Version

This realization is a character-led dark symphonic power-metal opera with complete
version lyrics in `lyrics/NN.txt`. It shares the reduced-cast dramatic script of the
Codex oratorio at this revision, but directs every phrase through a physical metal
band: riffs argue, drums change allegiance, and character motifs live in electric
lead, organ, brass, and violin.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor:** dark mythic symphonic power metal with memorable physical riffs,
  live-feeling drums, cathedral scale, and theatrical soloists.
- **Shared core:** qualified distorted electric rhythm guitars, overdriven electric
  lead, electric bass, double-kick, war toms, organ, low brass, strings, bells, and
  disciplined choirs. Quiet chapters withhold this weight rather than flattening it.
- **Character motion:** Zeus descends through organ, brass, and half-time weight;
  Prometheus rises through violin and electric lead; humanity grows from heartbeat
  to choir; Future Intelligence begins crystalline and gradually inherits the band.
- **Production signature:** hard transient attack, tight low end, intelligible words,
  wide but controlled choirs, and enough depth for opposed voices to remain distinct.

## Vocal and lyric doctrine

- Each track uses two or three vocal forces. Named characters carry the opera;
  choirs count as one force and appear only when collective belief is dramatic action.
- Forms follow the scene: processional, creation duet, accusation, creed duel,
  passacaglia judgment, doom masque, panic scherzo, relay fugue, nocturne, chaconne,
  and suspended finale. Metal energy does not require one repeated song template.
- The version owns its lyric files. Future revisions may diverge them track by track
  from any other version while retaining the root story, safeguards, motifs, seams,
  and required keywords.
- Proven Suno formatting and generation practices remain mandatory.

## Act dynamics

- **00–03:** buried pressure becomes full battle force, then collapses to intimate
  creation without losing the mythic band identity.
- **04–08:** accusation, creed duel, legal rupture, doom procedure, and frantic
  consequence each use a different riff function rather than one shared wall.
- **09–10:** mechanical oppression gives way to an accelerating ember relay.
- **11–14:** piano and crystalline pulse awaken the ancient motifs; the band returns
  for the final choice, then withdraws into an unresolved open chord.

## Musical freedom

Tempo, meter, key route, riff density, metal weight, and orchestral balance are chosen
per song. The root key families remain motif memory, not fixed law. Concrete sounds
and phrases preserve seams even when the musical handoff is a deliberate rupture.

## Generation

Per-track settings and excludes live in `GENERATION.md`. Style prompts use the
validated layered directing format, qualified electric-guitar language, explicit
two/three-force cast fences, and a final production line. Prompts remain below 1,000
characters; lyrics remain below 5,000.

## Comparison focus

Judge this version on riff identity, voice separation, dynamic contrast, physical
band interplay, and whether recurring motifs transform with the moral argument.
