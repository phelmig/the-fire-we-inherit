[Intro: the hidden heartbeat enters the orchestra; thunder approaches in measured brass][Intro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, urgent recitative]
Zeus opened the prison.
The swallowed children rose.
But ten years of mountain war
could not break Kronos' throne.

Then Prometheus the Foreseer
left the elder host behind.
He brought no claim upon the thunder—
only counsel for the fight.

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, ascending and precise]
Stone against stone
only buries the field.
Free every power he divided.
Let foresight choose
where strength shall fall.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, noble descending phrases]
Stand beside me.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, unwavering]
I stand beside the future.

[Chorus][ATHENA, ZEUS, AND PROMETHEUS — Female contralto, male basso, and male high tenor, sharply separated registers, processional unity]
Open the gate. Unseal the dawn.
No child is tribute to a crown.
Let strength answer for the lives it saves.
Let no throne feed upon a cage.

[Verse 2][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, accelerating above orchestral battle]
The buried powers were freed.
They forged the thunderbolt.
One hand became a storm
because divided hands stood joined.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, unleashed but controlled]
Kronos—
I am the breath you tried to end.
Hear every swallowed future
answer in my storm!

[Chorus][ATHENA, ZEUS, AND PROMETHEUS — Female contralto, male basso, and male high tenor, sharply separated registers, processional unity]
Open the gate. Unseal the dawn.
No child is tribute to a crown.
Let strength answer for the lives it saves.
Let no throne feed upon a cage.

[Breakdown: one thunderbolt; bells answer across the suddenly silent mountain]

[Bridge][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, solemn and sincere]
No child beneath my sky
will live inside a cage.
No child of tomorrow
shall be buried
for the safety of today.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, warm warning]
Then let the law remember
why it was raised.

A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, unguarded]
Counsel me
when thunder cannot see.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, gentle ascent]
Hear me
when the future speaks.

[Instrumental Break: solo violin rises through the victory processional, unresolved]

[Chorus][ATHENA, ZEUS, AND PROMETHEUS — Female contralto, male basso, and male high tenor, sharply separated registers, radiant processional unity]
Open the gate. Unseal the dawn.
No child is tribute to a crown.
Let strength answer for the lives it saves.
Let no throne feed upon a cage.

[Outro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, quietly observant]
The world was ordered.
It was not complete.
