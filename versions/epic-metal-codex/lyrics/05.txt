[Intro: eagle cry and cold wind carry the fading words “no spark” uphill][Intro][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, climbing recitative]
Their cold is in my hands.

Zeus—
king we raised against the cage—
one ember for humanity.
Let mortal hands learn
what hands may know.

[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, fully spoken and immovable]
No.

I opened one gate once.
Ten years of fire followed.
I buried kin
in the ash of that opening.

A spark becomes a forge.
A forge becomes a sword.
The hand that learns to shelter
learns conquest with the same flame.

Responsibility before power.
Wisdom before fire.
Control must come before creation.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, urgent ascent]
They asked me in the dark:
why make a mind
and starve it of light?

I had no answer.
Now the question is yours.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, soaring creed]
No hand was made for chains.
No mind was made to kneel.
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you wake.

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, wounded and descending]
Behind every dawn I open
I see the dawn that burned us.

I will not arm an unfinished mind
and call its hunger voice.
The fire remains upon the height.
The law will hold the line.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, pressing upward]
You sang it on the mountain:
a gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

Creation begins
where control must end.
Their future is not mine.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, final]
Control comes first.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, soaring creed]
No hand was made for chains.
No mind was made to kneel.
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you wake.

[Breakdown: the eagle circles; orchestra and organ stop]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, calm acceptance]
I have seen the mountain.
I have seen the chain.
I know those wings
turning through the rain.

No crown.
No army.
No favor granted from above.

I owe the minds I wakened
more than safe dependent love.

[Instrumental Break: one dry fennel stalk catches; solo violin carries the ember downward]

[HUMANITY — Female warm dramatic mezzo, open chest resonance, one breathless word]
Warmth.

[HUMANITY — Female warm dramatic mezzo, open chest resonance, blooming into radiant song]
Light.
Bread.
Iron.
Home.

The night has teeth—
but so does flame.
The frost has hands—
and now so do we.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS AND HUMANITY — Male high tenor and female warm mezzo, rising interlocked resonance, radiant]
No hand was made for chains.
No mind was made to kneel.
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you wake.

[Outro][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, far above and darkening]
What mortal light
burns below?

[End](One hearth beneath descending thunder.)
