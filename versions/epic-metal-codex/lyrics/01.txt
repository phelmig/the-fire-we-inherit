[Intro: thunder dissolves into a contrabass ground and distant shield resonance][Intro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, measured recitative]
I am Athena.
I did not witness the first dawn.
I learned its shape
from the wounds each throne passed on.

Chaos had no gate.
Then earth took form,
then sky,
then Uranus raised a crown
and feared the life beneath it.

[Verse 1][SUCCESSION CHOIR — Male low chamber choir, buried chest resonance, rigid homophony]
Close the gate.
Seal the dawn.
Bury the future
before it is born.

[Chorus][ATHENA AND SUCCESSION CHOIR — Female contralto over male low choir, opposed resonance, grave and inevitable]
Whatever is buried returns.
Whatever is silenced learns weight.
A throne may wall tomorrow away—
tomorrow remembers the gate.

[Verse 2][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, darkening recitative]
Kronos broke the elder prison.
The sky felt pain.
He took the crown,
heard an heir would end him,
and made the wound again.

He swallowed every child to come,
each unwritten name,
and called the stillness peace.

[SUCCESSION CHOIR — Male low chamber choir, devouring chest resonance, slow descending chant]
Close the gate.
Seal the age.
Keep every heir
inside the cage.

[Chorus][ATHENA AND SUCCESSION CHOIR — Female contralto over male low choir, opposed resonance, grave and inevitable]
Whatever is buried returns.
Whatever is silenced learns weight.
A throne may wall tomorrow away—
tomorrow remembers the gate.

[Breakdown: the ground bass stops; one heartbeat beneath mountain wind]

[Bridge][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, intimate and exact]
But one child vanished
from the hunger of the throne.

Shields drowned the infant cry.
A mountain kept the breath.

His name was Zeus.
He learned the world
behind a closed gate.
He learned what fear
will make of fate.

[Instrumental Break: shield resonance gathers around the heartbeat; the ground bass returns one step higher]

[Chorus][ATHENA AND SUCCESSION CHOIR — Female contralto over male low choir, opposed resonance, fuller and inevitable]
Whatever is buried returns.
Whatever is silenced learns weight.
A throne may wall tomorrow away—
tomorrow remembers the gate.

[Outro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, nearly whispered]
The hidden child kept breathing.

[End](The heartbeat becomes approaching thunder.)
