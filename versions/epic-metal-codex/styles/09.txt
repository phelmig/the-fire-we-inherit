Oppressive symphonic heavy power metal funeral march, grieving into mechanical force, 72 rising to 108 BPM, E minor.
Broken bell, piano, cello, organ, slow toms, then chugging distorted electric rhythm guitars, anvils, low brass, mechanical double-kick.
Opening: Athena begins immediately over wind and hammer. Guardian descends with the heavy march. Maker rises in bright tenor phrases repeatedly cut off by the riff. Chorus: orchestra and band harden the warning into machinery, vast but never triumphant. Bridge crushes into anvils. Final chorus slows; forge dies to one ember.
Voices: exactly three — icy female contralto Athena; weathered male bass-baritone Guardian; young bright male tenor Maker; no other singers.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, oppressive weight, clear confrontation, punchy machinery, tiny final ember.
