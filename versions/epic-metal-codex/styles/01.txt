Severe sacred symphonic-metal ground-bass ballad, claustrophobic, 68 BPM, D minor.
Bowed basses, cello, cathedral organ, sub-bass, low brass, bells, shield strikes, ritual floor toms.
Opening: Athena begins immediately over fading thunder. Recitative: sparse and exact above a descending organ ground. Choir: buried male homophony grows devouring without full band release. Chorus: contralto rises against the bass. Breakdown: ground vanishes around one heartbeat. Outro: distant thunder approaches.
Voices: two — icy female contralto Athena; low male Succession Choir changing from buried to devouring; no other singers.
Sacred studio quality, professional mastering, cavernous depth, natural low strings, intelligible narration, monumental restraint.
