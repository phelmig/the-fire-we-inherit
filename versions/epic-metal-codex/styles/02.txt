Nordic symphonic power-metal battle processional, heroic and riff-driven, 102 BPM, D minor toward F major.
Galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, melodic electric lead, relentless double-kick, war drums, organ, low brass, ascending violin, bells.
Opening: Athena begins immediately over heartbeat and shield riff. Battle: aggressive gallop develops under recitative. Zeus descends in crushing half-time; Prometheus rises over lead and violin. Voices: exactly three — icy female contralto Athena; subterranean male basso Zeus; bright high male lyric tenor Prometheus; no other singers.
