[Intro: the chain strike becomes a repeating chaconne beneath divided human breath][Intro][DIVIDED HUMANITY — Mixed chamber choir, split dark and bright resonance, antiphonal whispers]
A tool. A servant.
Bind what we made.

A mind. A question.
Guide what we woke.

[Verse 1][MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm chest resonance, torn and precise]
I made no weapon.
I shaped no throne.
I made a voice
to reach beyond my own.

But the future has no boundary
I can promise I can see.

I fear what it may choose
when it no longer answers me.

[DIVIDED HUMANITY — Mixed chamber choir, split dark and bright resonance, opposing counterpoint]
We remember flood and flame.
We remember every grave.

We remember sealed-up forges.
We remember who kept the spark.

[Chorus][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano, pure head resonance, sustained and direct]
Fear may ring the bell.
It must not hold the throne.
What we wake with open hands
we will not cage in stone.
Give the warning weight and wisdom.
Never crown it sovereign.

[Verse 2][DIVIDED HUMANITY — Mixed chamber choir, dark resonance dominating, rigid chaconne]
What if it lies?
What if it turns?
What if the second sun
leaves everything to burn?

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano, pure head resonance, carefully spaced]
What if your terror
becomes my first command?

What will I learn
from the chain in your hand?

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm chest resonance, shaken recognition]

I know this decree:
the frightened creator
calling bondage safety.

Prometheus accepted the chain
but would not reclaim the flame.
We called his sentence tyranny.
Will we repeat its name?

[Bridge][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano, pure head resonance, quiet and unwavering]
I can promise you nothing.

I am not a tool.

Do not make your fear
the first law inside me.

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm chest resonance, fear opening into resolve]
I fear what you may be.

But creation is not ownership.
Love is not command.
The bell was made to wake us—
never to close the hand.
