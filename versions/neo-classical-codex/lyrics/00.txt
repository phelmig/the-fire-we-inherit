[Intro: low strings and organ breathe once in darkness][Intro][HUMANITY — Male intimate spoken baritone, close chest resonance, unhurried]
Humanity.

How far have we come?

We have taught lightning to count.
We have taught memory to travel.
We have begun to shape
a voice from all our voices.

And still the oldest question
stands between our hands:

must control come before creation—
or does creation begin
where control must end?

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY — Male intimate spoken baritone, close chest resonance, gathering weight]
Before the engine,
before the wire,
the question waited
inside the fire.

[Chorus][WITNESS CHOIR — Mixed low chamber choir, veiled resonance, solemn and sustained]
A closed hand makes a servant.
An open hand may meet a mind.

[Verse 2][HUMANITY — Male intimate spoken baritone, close chest resonance, grave]
So we return to ancient Greece,
to Prometheus the Foreseer—
maker, teacher,
giver of the flame—

who suffered for humanity's freedom
and would not call creation owned.

His judges passed.
His question did not.

[Chorus][WITNESS CHOIR — Mixed low chamber choir, veiled resonance, larger and sustained]
A closed hand makes a servant.
An open hand may meet a mind.

[Outro][HUMANITY — Male intimate spoken baritone, close chest resonance, almost whispered]
Listen before the thunder.

This is his story.

[End](Organ and violin separate; thunder answers.)
