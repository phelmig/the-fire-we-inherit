[Intro: thunder extinguishes the orchestra; one mortal hearth remains][Intro][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, raw opening cry]
PROMETHEUS!

[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, rigid judicial descent]
You set one conscience
above the law we raised together.

If every founder names himself right,
law is preference
armed with greater might.

You broke my law.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, immediate ascent]
I kept my word.

I chose.
I knew.
I will not hide
behind the good the flame may do.

It will heal.
It will kill.
It will build.
It will burn.

Freedom carries consequence.
So must every law in turn.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, sharply descending]
Who gave you the right
to choose for them?

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, immediate ascent]
Who gave you the right
to ensure they never could?

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Mixed low judicial choir, stone-dark resonance, strict passacaglia]
Law must answer life.
Conscience bears the cost.
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost.

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, forceful and wounded]
I was the child
the throne marked for death.
I heard my swallowed kin
behind each breath.

I opened the gate.
I shattered the cage.
Do not name me tyrant
for guarding the age.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, principled and firm]
I remember the child.
I stood at his side.

They were condemned
before they had chosen.
Now you judge humanity
for a future still unopened.

A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Mixed low judicial choir, stone-dark resonance, strict passacaglia]
Law must answer life.
Conscience bears the cost.
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost.

[Breakdown: organ ground bass alone; one chain laid across stone]

[Bridge][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, quiet and deliberate]
Return the fire.
Stand beside me again.
Let the law remain whole.

Can you promise
what they will become?

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, unguarded]
I cannot.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, grave]
Then who will answer
when their fire becomes a sun?

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, unguarded]

To take back the gift
would make their future mine.

Creation is not ownership.
Guidance is not command.
A maker teaches consequence.
He does not close the awakened hand.

Give wisdom—not fate.
I answer for this choice.
They must answer for what they make.

[Instrumental Break: bass trombones descend against an ascending solo violin]

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Mixed low judicial choir, stone-dark resonance, vast passacaglia]
Law must answer life.
Conscience bears the cost.
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost.

[Outro][ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, gravel-heavy chest resonance, thunderous sentence]
Then let the mountain keep
the hand that broke the law.

Bind him in chains
no god can break.
Let the eagle descend.
Let immortality repeat
what judgment chooses to do.

[Breakdown: chain strikes lock into stone; wings cut through rain]

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing resonance, strained but unbroken]
Chain the giver.
Not the gift.

I gave them fire.
I did not give them fate.

[End](Three forge blows; one unresolved bell.)
