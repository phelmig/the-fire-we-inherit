[Intro: one distant hammer lands; wind carries a broken bell into a funeral march][Intro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, mournful recitative]
After the cry
came quieter things:

a lock on the grain,
a mark on the door,
a ruler who promised
danger would enter no more.

Fear found a language.
It learned how to stay.
It asked for protection—
then asked who must obey.

[Verse 1][GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, grieving chest resonance, slow descending phrases]
I am the Guardian.
I buried what the vessel cost.

Do not ask me to forget
the names the fever lost.

Remember the vessel.
Remember the cry.
Every open hand
invites an open sky.

[Chorus][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, grave over funeral march]
The warning became law.
The bell became a throne.
Fear closed the hand it meant to guard
and called the silence home.

[Verse 2][MAKER — Male bright young lyric tenor, clean rising resonance, urgent and idealistic]
I am a maker.
The vessel was not our crime.
The trap was forged above us.
Do not make its fear our time.

Let the forge teach consequence.
Let the question answer back.
A lock can guard a doorway—
it cannot teach a hand restraint.

[GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, grieving chest resonance, sincere and immovable]
Seal the forge.

Not from hatred.
From love
of what remains.

[MAKER — Male bright young lyric tenor, clean rising resonance, quiet defeat]
From love,
you build the cage.

[Chorus][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, grave over funeral march]
The warning became law.
The bell became a throne.
Fear closed the hand it meant to guard
and called the silence home.

[Breakdown: the funeral march hardens into measured anvils and mechanical breath]

[Bridge][GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, grieving chest resonance, commanding without triumph]
Chain the bellows.
Still the song.
No new flame
until the danger has a name.

[MAKER — Male bright young lyric tenor, clean rising resonance, nearly buried]
Then who will learn
to name it?

[Instrumental Break: anvils slow; solo cello carries the Maker's rising phrase and is cut off]

[Chorus][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, slower and final]
The warning became law.
The bell became a throne.
Fear closed the hand it meant to guard
and called the silence home.

[Outro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure resonance, close and quiet]
The forge fell silent.
The songs of making stopped.

But under ash,
where no law looked,
one ember kept its breath.

[End](Wind through the cold forge; one ember pulses like a heartbeat.)
