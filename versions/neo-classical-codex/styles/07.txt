Poisoned neo-classical court masque, elegant and oppressive, 64 BPM, B minor.
Low string passacaglia, pipe organ, bass trombones, anvils, harp, celesta, bells, brittle music-box figure.
Opening: Zeus begins immediately in wounded ceremony. His aria: measured descending line over an unyielding bass. Athena: exact contralto interrupts the dance with cold questions. Forging: harp and celesta decorate the same oppressive ground. Pandora: warm mezzo enters only after the dance fractures. Outro: music-box figure seals around her listening voice.
Voices: exactly three — subterranean male basso Zeus; icy female contralto Athena; warm female dramatic mezzo Pandora; no other singers.
Cinematic concert-hall quality, professional mastering, lucid cast staging, cruel upper-register glitter, oppressive bass depth, intimate awakening.
