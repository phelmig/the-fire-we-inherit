Tense neo-classical oratorio creed duet, urgent and severe, 90 BPM, E minor.
Rigid organ, bass trombones, martial timpani, driving string ostinato, ascending solo violin, harp harmonics, one ember strike.
Opening: Prometheus begins immediately over eagle wind. Zeus: fully spoken, descending, immovable above ground bass. Prometheus: fully sung in rising long lines; exchanges compress. Chorus: tenor creed opens the harmony. Theft: absolute silence. Humanity enters as one warm mezzo bloom over radiant strings. Outro: descending thunder cuts the light.
Voices: exactly three — spoken subterranean male basso Zeus; sung bright high male tenor Prometheus; warm female dramatic mezzo Humanity; no other singers.
Cinematic concert-hall quality, professional mastering, maximum male contrast, fierce orchestral attack, radiant human bloom, precise silence.
