Severe neo-classical sacred ground-bass ballad, primordial, 68 BPM, D minor.
Bowed contrabasses, cello, pipe organ, bass trombones, shield resonance, sparse timpani, one hidden heartbeat.
Opening: Athena begins immediately over the fading thunder. Recitative: exact, spacious, held above a descending ground. Choir: rigid homophony that grows from buried breath to devouring weight. Chorus: contralto rises against the unchanged bass. Breakdown: ground vanishes around the heartbeat. Outro: distant thunder approaches.
Voices: two — icy female contralto Athena; low male Succession Choir changing from buried to devouring; no other singers.
Sacred concert-hall quality, professional mastering, cavernous acoustic depth, exact diction, natural percussion, monumental restraint.
