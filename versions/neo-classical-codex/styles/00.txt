Dark neo-classical spoken oratorio invocation, restrained, 58 BPM, D minor.
Contrabass pedal, pipe organ, distant bell, solo violin, faint glass harmonics.
Opening: Humanity begins immediately, close and dry. Question: organ descends while violin rises, leaving space around every clause. Chorus: low chamber choir enters only for the two-line witness. Outro: all resonance collapses into thunder.
Voices: two — one intimate male spoken baritone as Humanity; one low mixed chamber choir as the witness; no other singers.
Intimate concert-hall quality, professional mastering, close speech, natural low resonance, disciplined choir depth, deep preserved silence.
