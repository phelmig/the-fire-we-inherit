Heroic neo-classical battle processional, relentless acoustic force, 102 BPM, D minor opening toward F major.
Aggressive staccato strings, taiko, timpani, gran cassa, shield strikes, horns, bass trombones, organ, bells, ascending solo violin.
Opening: Athena begins immediately over heartbeat and measured brass. Battle recitative: strings drive in developing counterpoint. Zeus: massive descending phrases. Prometheus: clear rising melody. Chorus: all three registers join without becoming a choir. Oath: processional broadens; outro falls to unresolved violin and wet clay.
Voices: exactly three — icy female contralto Athena; subterranean male basso Zeus; bright high male lyric tenor Prometheus; no other singers.
Cinematic concert-hall quality, professional mastering, fierce acoustic transients, huge dynamic range, clean register separation, lucid words.
