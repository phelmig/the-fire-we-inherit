# The Fire We Inherit — Neo-Classical Codex Version

This realization is a character-led symphonic oratorio with its own complete lyric
set in `lyrics/NN.txt`. The root concept supplies the story, dramatic jobs,
character safeguards, motifs, and required verbal inheritance; this version is free
to reshape every musical and lyrical surface around acoustic orchestral drama.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor:** dark neo-classical symphonic oratorio—long-breathed arias, tense
  recitative, developing counterpoint, and concert-hall dynamics.
- **Shared palette:** divided strings, solo violin and cello, contrabass, woodwinds,
  horns, bass trombones, timpani, gran cassa, taiko, pipe organ, bells, harp,
  celesta, and selective cathedral choir. Future tracks add piano, glass harmonics,
  and crystalline synthesis without abandoning the ancient orchestra.
- **Motif motion:** Zeus descends; Prometheus rises; law repeats as ostinato;
  humanity grows from breath to ensemble; the new mind widens a crystalline interval
  until it can carry violin, bell, fire, and forge motifs itself.
- **Production signature:** close intelligible soloists inside natural orchestral
  depth, disciplined low end, real transients, and silence preserved as music.

## Vocal and lyric doctrine

- Each track uses no more than **two or three vocal forces**. Prefer named character
  singers—solo arias, duets, and trios—over an all-purpose narrator. A choir counts
  as one force and appears only where collective belief is the drama.
- Lyrics are written for the composition. They may be through-composed, refrain-led,
  antiphonal, or recitative-heavy; they do not inherit martial anthem structure.
- Every track still has a memorable recurring verbal or melodic center. Repeated
  chorus text stays verbatim when used, while scene-specific retorts remain unique.
- The album sequence may carry an audible character introduction forward. Each track
  nevertheless includes enough sung context to remain emotionally intelligible.
- Directions remain lean and renderable. Suno structural tags, fused compact intros,
  explicit vocal descriptions, cast fences, character limits, and `[End]` remain
  mandatory.

## Musical freedom

The canonical key families remain useful motif memory but are not a prison. Every
track may change tempo, meter, orchestral weight, harmonic route, or metal content
when its lyric form demands it. Distorted electric instruments are optional colors,
never a version-wide obligation. Musical seams reuse concrete sounds or phrases even
when tempo and key turn sharply.

## Generation

Per-track settings and excludes live in `GENERATION.md`. Styles follow the validated
layered directing format, stay below 1,000 characters, and use cast declarations with
two or three fenced vocal forces. Lyrics stay below 5,000 characters and normally
target 1,800–3,000.

## Comparison focus

Judge this version on character clarity, expressive counterpoint, lyric-to-form fit,
the meaningful transformation of recurring motifs, and whether the orchestra carries
the philosophy without collapsing every track into the same cinematic crescendo.

The recovered original Neo playlist is an artistic ancestor, not a lyric template.
This realization selectively restores its strongest philosophical exchanges and motif
payoffs while retaining the Codex version's reduced two-or-three-force casting,
person-led arias, compact refrains, and explicit Suno structure.
