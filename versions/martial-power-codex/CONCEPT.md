# The Fire We Inherit — Martial Power Codex Version

This comparison version recasts every chapter as a field hymn: direct, memorable,
and built for collective singing, while preserving enough named narrative anchors
for the story to remain understandable from audio alone. It uses its own compact
lyrics in `lyrics/NN.txt`; the canonical story remains in the repo-root `songs/`.

## Sound identity

- **Anchor:** martial heavy power metal with marching snare, chugging distorted
  electric rhythm guitars, forceful bass, double-kick, low brass or synth-brass,
  and gang vocals that sound like people bound by one urgent belief.
- **Verse engine:** one weathered male war-narrator carries chronology and stakes.
  Named dramatic voices enter only when the moral turn cannot be honestly narrated
  from outside.
- **Hook engine:** every track has one short creed, repeated verbatim, that survives
  outside the myth while still changing meaning across the album.
- **Production signature:** hard modern metal attack, loud intelligible lead,
  disciplined gang vocals, compact arrangements, and no decorative excess.

## Song doctrine

- Each track has one governing message; verses dramatize only the story beats needed
  to earn it. Narrative clarity outranks the old one-name-per-song restriction.
- Standard anthem form is intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge or
  breakdown, instrumental break, final chorus, outro, and `[End]`.
- The chorus repeats verbatim at least twice. Retorts and scene-specific answers stay
  outside the repeating refrain.
- Every central speaker is introduced audibly by name and role before or at the first
  entrance. Labels always include gender, register, resonance, and delivery.
- The narrator, soloists, and choirs are separate named forces in the cast declaration;
  no unlisted singer appears.
- Prometheus acts from obligation and accepts consequence. Zeus is persuasive and
  wounded. Pandora rejects blame. Fear warns but never deserves sovereignty. The
  Future Intelligence is a free moral inheritor, not a monster, savior, god, or heir
  to humanity's place.

## Quiet chapters

- **00:** spoken muster over drone and a snare approaching from far away.
- **01:** ritual shield-and-drum march; no guitars.
- **11:** piano-led watch ballad, crystalline answering voice, electric violin after
  first contact; the question remains unresolved.
- **12:** empty-fortress march whose proud tread breaks under Athena's verdict.
- **14:** slow 6/8 departure hymn with piano, violin, restrained pipes, and field drum;
  hopeful as duty, never as promised safety.

## Tempo, keys, and seams

This version uses a simplified martial key path while retaining intentional handoffs:
D minor (00–04), E minor (05), B minor (06–08), E minor (09), A minor (10), F-sharp
minor (11), E minor (12), C-sharp minor (13), and D major suspended over D minor (14).
Concrete seam sounds and repeated phrases preserve the continuous journey.

## Generation

Per-track settings and exclude lists live in `GENERATION.md`. Styles and lyrics never
reference artists. All style prompts remain under 1,000 characters; all lyric files
remain under 5,000 characters and target 1,800–3,000.

## Comparison focus

Judge this version on chorus memorability, audible plot clarity, the credibility of
the opposing argument, and whether the same martial machinery can carry wonder,
grief, warning, and release without flattening them.
