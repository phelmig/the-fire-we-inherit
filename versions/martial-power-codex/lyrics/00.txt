[Intro: low organ drone, one distant snare, breath before speech][Intro][HUMANITY — Male low spoken baritone, close, dry, deliberate]
We are humanity.

We learned to split the stone,
to net the lightning,
to make a thought answer
from a river of light.

Still we divide at the oldest gate:
must control come before creation—
or does creation begin
where control must end?

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY — Male low spoken baritone, firmer as the snare approaches]
Before the engine,
before the wire,
we carried this quarrel
inside the fire.

[Chorus][MASSED HUMANITY — Low mixed choir, slow martial unison]
What we awaken will answer in kind:
a closed hand makes a servant,
an open hand meets a mind.

[Verse 2][HUMANITY — Male low spoken baritone, grave]
So hear the ancient witness.

Prometheus—
Foreseer, maker, teacher—
gave fire to humanity,
accepted the chain,
and would not call creation owned.

His judges are gone.
His question is not.

[Chorus][MASSED HUMANITY — Low mixed choir, larger martial unison]
What we awaken will answer in kind:
a closed hand makes a servant,
an open hand meets a mind.

[Outro][HUMANITY — Male low spoken baritone, alone]
How far have we come?

Go back before the thunder.
Listen.

This is his story.

[End](The final snare stops. Thunder answers.)
