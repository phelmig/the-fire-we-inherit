[Intro: thunder kills the choir; one mortal hearth keeps beating][Intro][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, gravel-heavy chest resonance, raw furious cry]
PROMETHEUS!

[Verse 1][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, rigid descending phrases, controlled fury]
You crossed the guarded height.
You put your will above the law.
If every founder crowns his conscience,
law is only armed desire.

We raised one rule together
so strength would answer all.
One hand has chosen for the many.
You broke the common wall.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing tone, immediate]
I chose and will not hide.
The flame will heal and kill.
But keeping every hand dependent
is also sovereign will.

[Pre-Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Low mixed choir, rigid martial chant]
Who guards the fire?
Who guards the hand?
Principle rising!
Law taking stand!

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Massive mixed choir with both male leads, judicial and broad]
Law must answer life!
Conscience bears the cost!
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost!

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, forceful but wounded]
I was the child condemned unborn.
I heard my kin behind each breath.
I opened gates and broke the cage.
Do not call guarding life a death.

Return the fire.
Restore the line.
Stand at my side once more.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, sorrowful and rising]
To take their gift would make them mine—
the thing we fought before.

Creation is not ownership.
Guidance is not command.
A maker teaches consequence;
he does not close the hand.

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Massive mixed choir with both male leads, judicial and broad]
Law must answer life!
Conscience bears the cost!
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost!

[Breakdown: organ and chain pulse; the court holds one breath]

[Bridge][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, quiet and exact]
Can you promise
what they will become?

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, unguarded]
I cannot.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, descending]
Then who answers
when their spark becomes a sun?

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, ascending over violin]
Give wisdom—not fate.
I answer for this choice.
They must answer for what they make
when fire has found their voice.

[Instrumental Break: low brass and organ crush downward; electric lead and violin climb against them]

[Chorus][COURT CHOIR — Massive mixed choir with both male leads, judicial and broad]
Law must answer life!
Conscience bears the cost!
When a rule preserves the cage,
the purpose has been lost!

[Outro][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, thunderous decree]
Then bind the giver to the mountain.
Let the eagle make judgment renew.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, strained but unbroken]
Chain the giver.
Not the gift.

[End](Three forge blows. One unresolved bell.)
