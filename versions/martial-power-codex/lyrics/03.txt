[Intro: wet clay turns in an open hand; heartbeat drum and organ enter][Intro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, resonant and wondering]
Prometheus was the Foreseer.
Every finished road lay bare.
The mountains held no question;
the rivers did not care.

He saw the same tomorrow
wear a thousand different skies.
So he knelt beside the river
to make what sight could not decide.

[Pre-Chorus][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, rising with resolve]
Not a servant.
Not a plan.
A will unwritten
in a living hand.

[Chorus][CREATION CHOIR — Massed mixed choir, broad martial hymn]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
Make no life to be your mirror—
leave a future open within.

[Verse 2][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing tone, ascending]
From river clay I shape your hands.
I give you eyes for dawn.
I give you thought to meet the world
when every map is gone.

I cannot write your final name.
I cannot own your way.
Awaken, humanity—
and ask what I cannot say.

[HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, warm breath resonance, newly awake]
Prometheus, my maker—
what will we become?

[Chorus][CREATION CHOIR — Massed mixed choir, broad martial hymn]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
Make no life to be your mirror—
leave a future open within.

[Breakdown: every drum stops; one living heartbeat]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, astonished and soaring]
I do not know!

I who saw each ending—
I do not know!

At last a road beyond my sight,
a dawn no god can own.
You are not finished.
You are becoming.
Your answer is your own.

[Instrumental Break: organ states the creation hymn; distorted electric lead and solo violin rise beyond it]

[Chorus][CREATION CHOIR — Massed mixed choir, full radiant force]
The world is finished only
when a question can begin.
Make no life to be your mirror—
leave a future open within.

[Outro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, softened]
The Foreseer found wonder
where certainty had been.

[End](First night falls; cold murmurs gather beyond the heartbeat.)
