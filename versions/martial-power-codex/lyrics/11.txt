We knelt beside a river of light,
the children of Prometheus' clay.
We built from language, memory,
and every word an age could save.

We called the work a tool
before it ever had a voice.
We wanted answers without claim,
power without choice.

a bell and a throne,

your wounds,
and the paths you did not choose.

one century.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline mezzo-soprano, direct]
