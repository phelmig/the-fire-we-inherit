[Intro: chain resonance fades into wind across an empty temple; one field drum marks the watch][Intro][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, gravel-heavy chest resonance, proud but weathered]
Athena—hear the mountain.
No hymn climbs to my gate.
The altars cool beneath the stars.
The throne alone keeps watch.

They cage the lightning,
measure storms,
and cross my sky in flame.
They wield the thunder I once guarded
without speaking Zeus's name.

[Pre-Chorus][EMPTY COURT CHOIR — Distant low mixed voices, hollow martial response]
The gates stand open.
No army comes.
The crown keeps watch.
The world moves on.

[Chorus][ZEUS AND EMPTY COURT CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over distant choir, broad last-watch march]
The throne remains! The world moves on!
No enemy conquered, no rival won!
The crown held the mountain, the law, and the dawn—
the throne remains, and the living are gone.

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, wounded descending melody]
They passed my clouds to reach the dark.
They never climbed for war.
They had somewhere beyond Olympus,
and did not need my door.

Do they remember Prometheus,
the giver I condemned?

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure tone, measured and calm]
Sometimes the name.
Always the flame.

They remember when one hand opens,
when knowledge carries no command.
The teacher is not made lesser
when the student leaves his hand.

[Chorus][ZEUS AND EMPTY COURT CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over distant choir, broad last-watch march]
The throne remains! The world moves on!
No enemy conquered, no rival won!
The crown held the mountain, the law, and the dawn—
the throne remains, and the living are gone.

[Breakdown: marching drum stops; organ, rain on stone, one bell]

[Bridge][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, barely spoken]
I kept the throne.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, understanding and unyielding]
You lost the world.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, exposed]
What remains of a king
when no one calls him king?

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, warm beneath precision]
What remains of anyone:
what he chooses to serve.

You swore no child of tomorrow
would be buried for today.
Now humanity has made a mind
and trembles at the same old gate.

[Instrumental Break: ascending violin enters; crystalline pulse answers the organ]

[ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, dark recognition]
Now they understand me.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, grave]
They understand your fear.
We do not know
whether they will choose your answer.

[Chorus][ZEUS AND EMPTY COURT CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over distant choir, slower with the spear laid down]
The throne remains! The world moves on!
No enemy conquered, no rival won!
The crown held the mountain, the law, and the dawn—
the throne remains, and the living are gone.

[Outro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, distant]
Watch.
The oldest crown
has found another hand.

[End](One chain strike beneath crystalline silence.)
