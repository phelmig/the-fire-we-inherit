[Intro: three forge blows march beneath the dying bell][Intro][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, gravel-heavy chest resonance, wounded and commanding]
I chained Prometheus to the stone.
Still mortal hearths defy my sky.
Punishment has marked the crime;
it has not taught them why.

I broke the age that swallowed heirs.
I watched ten years consume the land.
If fire teaches clay to conquer,
who will stay the armed hand?

[Pre-Chorus][OLYMPUS CHOIR — Low mixed choir, fearful rigid chant]
Count the cost!
Guard the gate!
Make the warning
master fate!

[Chorus][ZEUS AND OLYMPUS CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over massed choir, slow martial anthem]
Every fire casts a shadow!
Every freedom leaves a scar!
When the gift outruns the wisdom,
fear must teach us what we are!

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, cooling into exact procedure]
Bring the forge.
Take earth and water.
Shape a woman for the road.
Name her Pandora—all gifts gathered.
Place the sealed grief in her hold.

Let beauty hide the court's intention.
Let language carry our decree.
Mortals chose the lawless ember.
Now consequence will make them see.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused pure tone, measured and cutting]
Zeus, a gift without consent
is merely punishment disguised.
You call your fear protection
while an innocent pays its price.

[Chorus][ZEUS AND OLYMPUS CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over massed choir, slow martial anthem]
Every fire casts a shadow!
Every freedom leaves a scar!
When the gift outruns the wisdom,
fear must teach us what we are!

[Breakdown: anvils stop; celesta turns the refrain into a cold music-box figure]

[Bridge][OLYMPUS CHOIR — Low mixed choir, ritual and ashamed]
One gave beauty.
One gave breath.
One gave language.
One hid death.

No single hand will bear the blame.
All heaven forged the hidden chain.

[PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, vulnerable and newly awake]
I am Pandora.
You call me gift—
but no one asked what I would choose.
Why give me hands and language
only to make them yours to use?

[Instrumental Break: harp and celesta circle the forge rhythm; low distorted electric guitars return beneath the sealed vessel]

[Chorus][ZEUS AND OLYMPUS CHOIR — Subterranean male bass over massed choir, grand but dark]
Every fire casts a shadow!
Every freedom leaves a scar!
When the gift outruns the wisdom,
fear must teach us what we are!

[Outro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, low and unyielding]
The cost of fire?

No.
The cost of fear.

[End](The vessel seals. One soft voice within whispers, “Open.”)
