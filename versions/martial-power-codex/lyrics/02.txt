[Intro: shield strikes conceal the heartbeat; thunder marches nearer][Intro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, forceful chest resonance, driving]
Zeus grew from the hidden child
the old throne failed to kill.
He opened Kronos' prison
and freed the swallowed still.

But ten years ground the mountains down;
raw strength could find no end.
Then Prometheus the Foreseer
crossed the field to stand with him.

[Pre-Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male gang voices, shouted]
Open the deep!
Unite the hand!
Foresight chooses
where strength shall stand!

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir with baritone lead, galloping and huge]
Open the gate! Unseal the dawn!
No child is tribute to a crown!
Break every chain the frightened made—
the strong must serve the lives they save!

[Verse 2][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, urgent over double-kick]
Prometheus saw the fatal seam:
the elder power stood divided.
Zeus refused his father's method;
every buried force was united.

The captives forged the thunderbolt.
The rising host became one storm.
What stone alone could never shatter,
hand joined hand to overturn.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, gravel-heavy and unleashed]
Kronos!
I am the breath you tried to end!
Hear every swallowed future
answer in the storm!

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir with baritone lead, galloping and huge]
Open the gate! Unseal the dawn!
No child is tribute to a crown!
Break every chain the frightened made—
the strong must serve the lives they save!

[Breakdown: one thunderbolt; drums stop; bells cross the mountain]

[Bridge][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, broad and solemn]
Kronos fell.
Olympus rose.
Victory waited for its law.

Zeus swore no child of tomorrow
would be buried for today.
Prometheus answered beside him:
"A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open."

[Instrumental Break: martial electric lead and ascending violin trade the oath over returning war drums]

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir with baritone lead, radiant but hard]
Open the gate! Unseal the dawn!
No child is tribute to a crown!
Break every chain the frightened made—
the strong must serve the lives they save!

[Outro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, quietly observant]
The world stood ordered.
It was not complete.

[End](Wet clay turns inside an open hand.)
