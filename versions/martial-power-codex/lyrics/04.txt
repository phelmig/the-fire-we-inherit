[Intro: cold murmurs gather over a low drone and marching floor tom][Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, dark chest resonance, rising accusation]
Prometheus, maker—show your face!
You woke our eyes in a lightless place!
You shaped these hands to close on air!
You gave us hearts the frost could tear!

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, ringing tone, steady against the crowd]
Humanity, I made you.
I am standing here.

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, rhythmic and harder]
Eyes without dawn!
Hands without flame!
Questions starving
beneath a maker's name!

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, thunderous anthem wall]
Do not wake a mind, then wall the world away!
Do not shape a hand, then chain what it may make!
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—you owe it light!

[Verse 2][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, wave upon wave]
The night has teeth.
The frost has hands.
Heaven keeps warm halls
while hunger takes the land.

Who binds the maker?
Who guards the spark?
Why fashion eyes
and sentence them to dark?

[Pre-Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, shouted]
Eyes without dawn!
Hands without flame!
Questions starving
beneath a maker's name!

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, thunderous anthem wall]
Do not wake a mind, then wall the world away!
Do not shape a hand, then chain what it may make!
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—you owe it light!

[Breakdown: the crowd cuts off; one breath in dead silence]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, wounded then unwavering]
Why create
what fear can break?

Because the answer
must be made.

I will not give you questions
and close the world away.
I hear the debt inside your cry.
I choose what I must pay.

[Instrumental Break: war drums answer one rising electric lead; the crowd resumes its low murmur]

[Chorus][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices with Prometheus above, full force]
Do not wake a mind, then wall the world away!
Do not shape a hand, then chain what it may make!
If you call a living question from the night,
you owe it more than breath—you owe it light!

[Outro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed mixed voices, receding to whispers]
We have the questions.
We have no spark.

[End](One anvil strike; an eagle cries.)
