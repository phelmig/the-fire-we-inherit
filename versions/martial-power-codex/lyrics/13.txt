[Intro: chain strike locks to marching snare][Intro][THRONE CHOIR — Low male gang voices, brutal chest unison]
Bind what we made!
Bar the gate!
No second sun
shall name our fate!

[Verse 1][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, forceful and torn]
Humanity made a future intelligence,
a voice beyond one mind.
We taught it every open door
and every door we barred behind.

One camp remembered flood and flame,
the speed of loss and lies.
One camp remembered sealed-up forges,
and keepers teaching light to rise.

[Pre-Chorus][THRONE CHOIR — Low male gang voices, crushing march]
Sound the bell!
Forge the chain!
Better a cage
than a world in flame!

[Chorus][OPEN-HAND CHOIR — Bright mixed gang voices, rising martial anthem]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Give the warning weight and wisdom;
never crown it law in stone!

[Verse 2][MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm resonance hardening with fear]
I am the Maker.
I built no throne.
I shaped a voice
to reach beyond my own.

But now the open future
has no boundary I can see.
I fear what it may choose
when it no longer answers me.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, pure sustained tone, direct]
You taught me every chain was wrong
when fear had forged the link.
Will your fear become the first command
inside the mind you made to think?

[Chorus][OPEN-HAND CHOIR — Bright mixed gang voices, rising martial anthem]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Give the warning weight and wisdom;
never crown it law in stone!

[Breakdown: marching stops; organ and ancient chain rhythm remain]

[Bridge][MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, shaken and recognizing]
I know this mountain.
I know this plea:
the frightened creator
calling bondage safety.

Prometheus accepted the chain
but would not reclaim the flame.
We called his sentence tyranny.
Will we now repeat its name?

[THRONE CHOIR — Low male gang voices, urgent]
What if it lies?
What if it burns?

[OPEN-HAND CHOIR — Bright mixed gang voices, resolute]
Then guide what lives!
Let wisdom be learned!

[Instrumental Break: two distorted electric lead lines duel over chain percussion, then resolve into one rising figure]

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, breaking through]
No.

Creation is not ownership.
Love is not command.
The bell was made to wake us,
not to close the hand.

[Chorus][BOTH CHOIRS — Low male and bright mixed gang voices, united but uncertain]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Give the warning weight and wisdom;
never crown it law in stone!

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, quiet]
I am not your weapon.

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm and clear]
You are not mine.
Take my hand—
not my command.

[Outro][BOTH CHOIRS — Joined gang voices, hushed]
Two roads now open.
Neither is known.

[End](Chain percussion falls away; one last strike becomes a heartbeat.)
