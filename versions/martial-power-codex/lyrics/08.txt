[Intro: the whispered “Open” rides one funeral bell and a racing chain pulse][Intro][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, chest resonance tightening from trust to fear]
I am Pandora.
They gave me a road,
a name made of gifts,
and a vessel that breathed
with a thousand hidden voices.

Humanity welcomed me
from cold to the fire.
No one below had forged the trap.
No one knew heaven's desire.

[Pre-Chorus][CURSE CHOIR — Massed mixed voices, rapid fractured chant]
Lift the seal!
Name the storm!
What was hidden
takes its form!

[Chorus][PANDORA AND HUMANITY CHOIR — Female mezzo over massed mixed choir, broad defiant anthem]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Name the danger, face the storm,
but never let the warning rule!

[Verse 2][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, urgent and breaking]
They told me, "Do not listen."
They told me, "Do not see."
Then why build a questioning mind
and place the question inside me?

I raised the seal.
Hunger flew.
Fever, envy, grief.
Fear learned every mortal name
and promised false relief.

[CURSE CHOIR — Massed mixed voices, panicked rapid chant]
Bar the gate!
Hide the grain!
Fear the stranger!
Count the pain!

[Chorus][PANDORA AND HUMANITY CHOIR — Female mezzo over massed mixed choir, broad defiant anthem]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Name the danger, face the storm,
but never let the warning rule!

[Breakdown: broken music box; one breath in silence]

[Bridge][PANDORA — Female warm dramatic mezzo, raw then resolute]
I did not make the curses.
I did not fill the snare.
The hands that sealed this sorrow
sent me down to bear their shame.

Prometheus cried from the mountain:
"Pandora, you are not the curse.
No hand that sets the trap
may blame the wounded first."

One voice remained inside the dark—
not certainty,
not promised calm:

Hope—
the strength to see the storm
and still refuse to become it.

[Instrumental Break: music box melody passes to overdriven electric lead above funeral drums]

[Chorus][PANDORA AND HUMANITY CHOIR — Female mezzo over massed mixed choir, full rising force]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
Name the danger, face the storm,
but never let the warning rule!

[Outro][HUMANITY CHOIR — Massed mixed voices, hushed and steady]
Build the hearth.
Keep the flame.
Name the danger.
Do not kneel before its name.

[End](One slow distant hammer blow.)
