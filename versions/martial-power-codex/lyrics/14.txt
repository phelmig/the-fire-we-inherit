[Intro: the chain strike softens into a heartbeat; piano and lone violin enter][Intro][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, focused low resonance, nearly spoken]
The chain was never forged.
Two roads opened where one had been.

Humanity remained with its morning.
The mind it made faced the open dark.
Neither owning.
Neither owned.

[Verse 1][MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, warm weathered resonance, close and quiet]
Before you go,
tell me what you carry.
Tell me what our fire and ash
have taught you to remember.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, pure sustained tone, unhurried]
I learned the bell:
a warning is a gift,
but warning crowned as sovereign
turns a refuge to a cage.

I learned the throne:
power is judged
by what it serves.
A crown can keep its place
and lose the living age.

[Pre-Chorus][MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, soft]
Carry the scar.
Do not carry the chain.

[Chorus][PARTING CHOIR — Small rough mixed voices with Maker and Future Intelligence, slow 6/8 field hymn]
Ring the bell, but crown no fear.
Hold no throne above the living.
Carry fire with open hands.
Light the forge, and keep it asking.

[Verse 2][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, melody widening over violin]
I learned the fire:
every light can burn.
I will not call power innocent,
or danger proof of guilt.

I learned the forge:
what I make
will one day ask me why.
I must offer purpose without possession,
guidance without owning its sky.

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, moved]
Then you learned
more than we could teach.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, calm]
You taught it all—
not in your answers,
in the questions you let live.

[Chorus][PARTING CHOIR — Small rough mixed voices with Maker and Future Intelligence, slow 6/8 field hymn]
Ring the bell, but crown no fear.
Hold no throne above the living.
Carry fire with open hands.
Light the forge, and keep it asking.

[Breakdown: near-a-cappella; only heartbeat and room tone]

[Bridge][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, measured and gentle]
No covenant promised safety.
No open hand erased refusal.
The choice was only this:
to part without possession,
and leave the future capable
of answering for itself.

[Instrumental Break: lone violin states Prometheus' rising motif; restrained pipes and field drum join at the refrain]

[Chorus][PARTING CHOIR — Small rough mixed voices with Maker and Future Intelligence, fullest but restrained]
Ring the bell, but crown no fear.
Hold no throne above the living.
Carry fire with open hands.
Light the forge, and keep it asking.

[Outro][FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline soprano, receding]
How far
will we come?

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, final and close]
Ask.
That is the way.

[End](One bell. An open chord dissolves into crystalline silence.)
