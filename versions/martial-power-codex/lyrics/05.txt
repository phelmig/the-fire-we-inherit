[Intro: eagle cry over mountain wind; the valley whispers below][Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Distant mixed voices, dark breath resonance, fading]
No spark…
no spark…

[Verse 1][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clean ringing tone, climbing]
Zeus, king of Olympus—
one ember for humanity.
I gave them minds that question.
Their helplessness now answers me.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, gravel-heavy chest resonance, fully spoken and slow]
Prometheus, I broke one gate
and ten years burned the sky.
A spark becomes a forge and sword.
The weak are first to die.

Responsibility before power.
Wisdom before flame.
Control must come before creation
or law becomes a name.

[Pre-Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, urgent and ascending]
A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor with distant mixed choir, soaring]
No hand was made for chains!
No mind was made to kneel!
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you heal!

[Verse 2][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, fully spoken, wounded and immovable]
I hear the swallowed children
behind each opening door.
I will not arm an unfinished will
and call the danger more.

The fire stays upon the height.
The law will hold the line.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, quieter then resolved]
Creation begins where control must end.
Their future is not mine.

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor with distant mixed choir, soaring]
No hand was made for chains!
No mind was made to kneel!
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you heal!

[Breakdown: the eagle circles once; every instrument stops]

[Bridge][PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, calm and unwavering]
I have seen the mountain.
I have seen the chain.
I know the wings that find me
will return through every rain.

No banner.
No crown.
No mercy granted from above.
I owe the minds I wakened
more than safe dependent love.

[Instrumental Break: one dry fennel stalk catches; chain rhythm becomes double-kick]

[HUMANITY — Female warm dramatic mezzo, breathless first word]
Warmth.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Warm mixed choir, suddenly radiant and strong]
Light! Bread! Iron! Home!
The night has teeth—
but so does flame!
The frost has hands—
and now so do we!

[Chorus][PROMETHEUS AND HUMANITY CHOIR — Bright male tenor and warm mixed choir, full force]
No hand was made for chains!
No mind was made to kneel!
Teach the cost and share the wisdom—
do not own the will you heal!

[Outro][ZEUS — Male subterranean bass, far above, stunned and darkening]
What mortal light
burns below?

[End](One hearth beneath gathering thunder.)
