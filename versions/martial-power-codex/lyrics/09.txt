[Intro: one bell, forge wind, one hammer falling on cold iron][Intro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, weathered chest resonance, mournful]
After Pandora's vessel,
fear chose quieter tools:
a lock upon the grain,
a mark upon the door,
a warning copied into rules.

The storm received a ledger.
The bell received a guard.
Protection asked obedience,
then named each question hard.

[Verse 1][GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, grieving resonance, slow and firm]
I am the Guardian.
I buried what the vessel cost.
I watched the open sky take names.
I will not lose what still is left.

[Pre-Chorus][LAW CHOIR — Low male gang voices, rigid shouted litany]
Lock the grain!
Mark the door!
Count the question!
Seal the store!

[Chorus][LAW CHOIR — Massed low male choir with Guardian above, vast and oppressive]
Remember the vessel! Remember the flame!
Close every forge that danger may claim!
Better the silence, the lock, and the wall
than one open hand that could ruin us all!

[Verse 2][MAKER — Male bright young lyric tenor, clean rising tone, urgent]
I am a maker.
The vessel was never our crime.
The trap came sealed from heaven—
do not make its fear our law.

[GUARDIAN — Male dark weathered bass-baritone, sincere and heavy]
I seal the forge from love,
not hatred of the hand.
Freedom's fire has buried homes.
Survival takes a stand.

[MAKER — Male bright young lyric tenor, nearly overwhelmed]
From love,
you build the cage.

[Chorus][LAW CHOIR — Massed low male choir with Guardian above, vast and oppressive]
Remember the vessel! Remember the flame!
Close every forge that danger may claim!
Better the silence, the lock, and the wall
than one open hand that could ruin us all!

[Breakdown: machinery slows; each final hammer lands farther apart]

[Bridge][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, dark and exact]
So warning hardened into doctrine.
The story of a trap
was turned against the hand it wounded.

The bellows chained.
The anvils cooled.
The songs of making stopped.
Fear won without a battlefield
and called the stillness law.

[Instrumental Break: organ and mechanical snare grind down into wind]

[Chorus][LAW CHOIR — Massed low male choir with Guardian above, slower and crushing]
Remember the vessel! Remember the flame!
Close every forge that danger may claim!
Better the silence, the lock, and the wall
than one open hand that could ruin us all!

[Outro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, close and quiet]
But under ash,
where no decree could hear,
one ember kept its breath.

[End](Wind through the cold forge; one faint ember heartbeat.)
