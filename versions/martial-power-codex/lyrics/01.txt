[Intro: thunder dissolves into shield strikes and a primordial drum][Intro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, low chest resonance, near-spoken and grave]
Athena learned the beginning
from every wound a throne passed on:
Chaos without border,
earth beneath the sky,
and Uranus fearing
the life that followed his.

He buried tomorrow under stone
and named the silence peace.

[Pre-Chorus][BURIED CHOIR — Low male gang voices, rigid descending chant]
Bar the gate!
Hide the dawn!
Keep what follows
from being born!

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir, slow pounding unison]
The throne that buries tomorrow
only teaches the buried to rise.
Fear may close every gateway—
it cannot command the skies.

[Verse 2][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, harder over rising drums]
Kronos broke his father's prison,
felt daylight on his face,
then heard an heir would end him
and rebuilt the same old cage.

He swallowed every child to come,
every unwritten name.
The hand that broke an elder chain
learned the elder fear again.

[Pre-Chorus][DEVOURING CHOIR — Low male gang voices, heavy and slow]
Bar the gate!
Hide the dawn!
Keep what follows
from being born!

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir, slow pounding unison]
The throne that buries tomorrow
only teaches the buried to rise.
Fear may close every gateway—
it cannot command the skies.

[Breakdown: drums vanish; one heartbeat beneath mountain wind]

[Bridge][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, intimate but tense]
But one child escaped the feast.

Shields drowned his infant cry.
A cave concealed his breathing.
No court recorded why.

His name was Zeus—
the hidden son,
raised behind the gate.

He learned before he learned the thunder
what fear will make of fate.

[Instrumental Break: shield strikes gather around the heartbeat, low brass rising]

[Chorus][WAR CHOIR — Massed male choir, full ritual force]
The throne that buries tomorrow
only teaches the buried to rise.
Fear may close every gateway—
it cannot command the skies.

[Outro][WAR NARRATOR — Male gruff baritone, nearly whispered]
The child kept breathing.
The mountain kept his name.

[End](The heartbeat becomes approaching thunder.)
