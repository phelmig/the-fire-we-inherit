Martial heavy power-metal accusation anthem, dark and crushing, 76 BPM, D minor.
Chugging distorted electric rhythm guitars, floor tom march, electric bass, low synth-brass, cold strings, sub impacts.
Opening: Humanity begins immediately as a low crowd murmur. Verses: each wave becomes more aggressive and riff-driven. Pre-chorus: clipped gang cadence. Chorus: crushing half-time vocal wall. Breakdown: dead silence around the lone tenor. Solo: one rising electric lead answered by war drums. Outro: whispers and eagle cry.
Voices: two forces — massed mixed Humanity chorus as the lead; lone bright high male tenor Prometheus; no other singers.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, huge intelligible crowd, hard low-end attack, stark silence, exposed contrasting tenor.
