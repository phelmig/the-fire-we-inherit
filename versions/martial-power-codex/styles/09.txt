Martial heavy power-metal law march, vast and oppressive, 76 rising to 116 BPM, E minor.
Broken bell, organ, cello, slow martial snare, then chugging distorted electric rhythm guitars, pounding bass, synth-brass wall, anvils, mechanical double-kick.
Opening: narrator begins immediately over wind and hammer. Guardian: grieving heavy march. Pre-chorus: rigid shouted litany. Chorus: crushing gang anthem, never triumphant. Maker: bright rising line almost buried. Bridge: machinery grinds down. Final chorus: slower and heavier. Outro: wind and ember heartbeat.
Voices: four forces — gruff male war-narrator; weathered male bass-baritone Guardian; bright young male tenor Maker; low male law choir; no other singers.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, oppressive modern weight, clear leads, brutal mechanical groove, cold depth, tiny final ember.
