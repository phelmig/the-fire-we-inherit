Martial progressive power-metal relay anthem, aggressive and accelerating, 128 BPM, A minor.
Palm-muted distorted electric rhythm guitars, relentless double-kick, marching snare, pounding bass, chain pulse, organ, synth-brass, soaring electric lead.
Opening: narrator begins immediately over ember pulse. Oath: hushed gang rhythm. Chorus: driving singalong. Each cycle becomes faster and more riff-driven. Guardian descends against the rising Maker. Breakdown: one counted ember. Solo: riff passes upward by register. Final chorus: maximum speed, then hard cut to pure tone.
Voices: four forces — gruff male war-narrator; massed mixed Keepers choir; weathered male bass-baritone Guardian; bright male tenor Maker; no other singers.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, exact acceleration, tight riffs, punchy drums, articulate gang vocals, explosive final cut.
