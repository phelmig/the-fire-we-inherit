Martial ritual power-metal oratorio, severe and primordial, 70 BPM, D minor.
Shield strikes, war drums, bowed basses, sub-bass, low brass, cathedral organ, bells.
Opening: narrator begins immediately over the fading thunder. Verses: near-spoken, locked to sparse drum strokes. Pre-chorus: descending gang chant. Chorus: broad pounding ritual march. Breakdown: drums vanish to a hidden heartbeat. Final refrain: shields surround the pulse; distant thunder takes the cadence.
Voices: four forces — gruff male war-narrator; buried low male choir; devouring low male choir; massed male war choir; no other singers.
Sacred studio quality, professional mastering, huge drum transients, cavernous low end, intelligible narration, monumental restrained choir.
