# 07 - Pandora’s Hand

## Style

```text
Dark symphonic power-metal tragedy, frantic double-time 164 feel over 82 BPM, B minor; fast chain percussion, urgent toms, driving double-kick, funeral bells, low organ, sharp tremolo strings, crushing distorted electric guitars, cathedral choir, sudden music-box breakdown, rapid call-and-response, huge fear-and-freedom refrain; momentum remains high until a brief fragile music-box ending and one distant hammer blow
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Intro: A whispered “Open” fades. One funeral bell. Chain strike — fast heartbeat drums. Wind around the vessel.]

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, warm and uncertain]
They gave me a road.
They gave me a name.
They gave me a vessel
that breathed like flame.

I came among strangers,
gold at my throat.
I carried their silence.
I carried their oath.

[HUMANITY — Mixed choir, quick and welcoming]
Come to the fire!
Come out of the cold!
No hand should wander
with no hand to hold!

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, tightening with fear]
Under the lid,
a thousand voices:
Open the door!
Obey their choices!

They said, “Do not listen.”
They said, “Do not see.”
Then why place this question
inside of me?

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, cold and certain]
Then let the mortals learn
what questions awaken.

Every fire has cost.
Every freedom
calls forth fear.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, spoken with grave wonder]
The price was set
before the question was asked.

Yet no mind born to wonder
will kneel before “do not see.”

Pandora chose to know.

She raised the seal.

[Thunder. Drum strike.]

[CHORUS — Mixed choir, rapid fractured cries]
From Olympus!
From the throne!
Came the curses
we would call our own!

Hunger! Fever! Grief! Despair!
Empty tables everywhere!

Envy! Jealousy! Gold in the hand!
Fear of the stranger! Fear of the land!

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, breaking]
I did not make them!
I did not give them names!
I opened a door—

but who had filled the flame?

[First refrain: Pandora above full choir]
Fear may ring the bell!
Fear may name the storm!
But greed will build a throne from it
and call that prison warm!

Envy feeds the watchful eye!
Jealousy draws every line!
What was sent to warn the living
must not rule the human mind!

[HUMANITY — Mixed choir, fast divided chant]
Keep the grain!
Bar the gate!
Someone rises—
someone takes!

Guard the child!
Guard the wall!
If we lose,
we lose it all!

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, precise and cutting]
Fear sees the wolf
beyond the flame.
It gives the danger
a face, a name.

But fed by envy,
fed by loss,
it counts each open hand
a cost.

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, defiant]
Do not make me
the face of your shame!
The ones who sealed it
must answer its name!

They forged the sorrow!
They fashioned the snare!
Then sent me below
to carry despair!

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, urgent from the mountain]
You are not the curse!
You were made into blame!
No hand that sets the trap
may call the wounded shame!

[Second refrain: heavier, chorus answers each line]
[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano]
Fear may ring the bell!

[CHORUS — Mixed choir]
Fear may name the storm!

[PANDORA AND CHORUS]
But greed will build a throne from it
and call that prison warm!

Envy feeds the watchful eye!
Jealousy draws every line!
What was sent to warn the living
must not rule the human mind!

[Breakdown: Music box, then silence.]

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, whispered]
One voice remains.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
Not certainty.

[PANDORA]
Hope?

[ATHENA]
The strength to see the storm—

and still refuse
to become it.

[Final refrain: full orchestra, rising key]
Fear may ring the bell!
Fear may name the storm!
But no fear shall build a throne
and call that prison warm!

Let the warning keep us living!
Let the open hand remain!
We will meet the world together—

we will not become the chain!

[Outro: the music box returns. The drums fall away.]

[HUMANITY — Mixed choir, hushed and resolute]
Build the hearth.
Keep the flame.
Name the danger.

Do not kneel
before its name.

[One slow, distant hammer blow.]
```
