# 01 - Before the Throne

## Style

```text
Epic sacred symphonic-metal overture, 78 BPM, D minor, solemn heavenly cathedral atmosphere; no guitars, no guitar riffs, no acoustic or Spanish-guitar elements; primordial sub-bass, bowed double basses, solo cello, pipe organ, church bells, harp, violins, low brass, sacred antiphonal choral harmonies, shield strikes, timpani, and restrained double-kick only at the final climax; cosmic creation and divine uprising, ominous yet radiant, like a forbidden liturgy beneath a newly made sky.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Thunder from 00 fades into a primordial drone.]

[Female icy contralto, nearly spoken]
In the beginning—

was Chaos.

No sky above.
No earth below.
No law to bind
what could not grow.

[Low male choir, distant]
Chaos.

[Female icy contralto]
Then earth took form.
Then sky was drawn.
Then came the first throne
before the dawn.

It gave the world
its shape and name—
then feared the life
from which it came.

[Low male choir, rigid and low]
Under stone.
Under sky.
What is born
must never rise.

[Female icy contralto]
The elder throne
sealed every gate.
It buried children
beneath its weight.

No future hand.
No newborn claim.
No living voice
to change its name.

[Low male choir]
Under earth!
Under stone!
Keep the future
unborn, unknown!

[Female icy contralto]
But Gaia hid a child
in mountain night.
Rhea gave him
to the mountain’s care.

The nymphs gave milk.
The shields gave thunder.
A hidden heir
grew strong in there.

[Low male choir, shield rhythm begins]
Strike the bronze!
Drown the cry!
Keep the hidden
heir alive!

[Male subterranean basso profundo, growing stronger]
I learned the first law
from a locked gate.
I learned what fear
will make of fate.

I was not born
to guard a grave.
I will break the gate.
I will unseal the age.

No child beneath my sky
will live inside a cage.

[First chorus: full choir, war drums, low brass, and cathedral organ]
Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
Let every hidden heir
be born!

From Chaos, stone,
and ancient night,
the world begins
beneath new light!

Break the wall!
Break the chain!
Let no throne
feed upon fear!

Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
The age of silence
falls away!

[Instrumental: shield strikes become double-kick rhythm.]

[Female icy contralto]
Then, from the elder age,
came Prometheus, the Foreseer.

Not for the thunder.
Not for the crown.

He gave his counsel
to the rising storm.

[Male bright high lyric tenor, calm and clear]
I have watched old kingdoms
close their hands around tomorrow.

I have seen fear
mistake a tomb for peace.

I stand with what is opening.
I stand with what may follow.

[Male subterranean basso profundo]
Then stand beside me.

[Male bright high lyric tenor]
I stand beside the future.

[Female icy contralto]
And the balance turned.

The storm met stone.
The old throne broke.
The prisons opened
beneath the smoke.

[Low male choir, transformed into war host]
Rise the buried!
Break the deep!
Let the waking
world be free!

[Male subterranean basso profundo, commanding but noble]
No law by hunger.
No peace by fear.
The strong shall answer
for what they hold here.

Let earth have seasons.
Let sea have shore.
Let no child hide
behind a locked door.

[Final chorus: full ensemble, massive]
Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
Let every hidden heir
be born!

From Chaos, stone,
and ancient night,
the world begins
beneath new light!

Break the wall!
Break the chain!
Let no throne
feed upon fear!

Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
The age of silence
falls away!

[Music falls to organ, cello, and a single rising violin.]

[Female icy contralto]
The old throne fell.

[Male subterranean basso profundo]
Let peace begin.

[Male bright high lyric tenor, distant]
Then let the world
become.

[Outro: a low heartbeat. The sound of wet clay in an open hand.]
```
