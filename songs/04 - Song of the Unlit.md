# 04 - Song of the Unlit


## Style

```text
A powerful accusing opera chorus slowly rising against one lone bright tenor — a crowd versus a single man, Dark dramatic opera scene, nearly a cappella at first, D minor, building from murmur to angry storm, A low drone, violins, cold staccato string ostinato, sparse cold strings, and one bell early; ritual percussion, deep cinematic booms, and low brass enter only as the accusation grows, the ostinato tightening beneath them, The chorus begins immediately as a circling murmur, swells in waves, and breaks into a massed accusatory wall; after the wall, a sub-drop into dead silence; the lone tenor answers against it, clear and unbowed, Ending: the crowd recedes to a hushed murmur; an eagle cries; the lone voice remains, quiet and resolved, Dark studio quality, professional mastering, massive modern low end, massed voices wide, the single voice close and human
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Intro][HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed voices, low murmured questions, slowly rising]
Who shaped these hands?
Who woke these eyes?
Who left us naked
under empty skies?

Maker—maker—
show your face.

[PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, stepping into the crowd, steady]
I shaped you.
I am here.

[HUMANITY CHORUS — Rising, wave upon wave]
You gave us eyes—
where is the dawn?
You gave us hands—
they close on wind!
You gave us questions—
the dark eats every one!

Why make a mind
and starve it of light?
Why make a heart
and leave it to the frost?

[PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, fighting to be heard]
I gave you all
I was allowed to give—

[HUMANITY CHORUS — Massed accusatory wall, thunderous, violins and cellos start]
Allowed?!
Who chains the maker?
Who holds the flame?

The night has teeth!
The frost has hands!
Your children freeze
while heaven warms its halls!

Why create
what fear can break?!

[Silence. The crowd breathes.]

[PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, quiet, wounded, unbowed]
Because the answer
must be made.

[HUMANITY CHORUS — Receding to a hushed murmur]
We have the cold.
We have the dark.
We have the questions.
We have no spark! [anvil strike]

[PROMETHEUS — Lone bright tenor, almost whispered, hardening into resolve]
Then I will not give you questions
and close the world away.

[End](Eagle Cry)
```
