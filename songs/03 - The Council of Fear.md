# 03 - The Council of Fear

## Style

```text
Dark operatic symphonic metal council scene, 74 BPM, B minor; ceremonial cathedral organ, low strings, hammering anvils, harp, bells, brass, divided choirs, and controlled heavy orchestration; individual gifts of craft, beauty, speech, and skill weave together into a glittering but ominous divine procession; starts restrained and ritualistic, rises into a massive accusatory choral refrain, ending on a sealed vessel and unresolved dread.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[The chain strike echoes. Olympus is silent.]

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, controlled and cold]
One hand has broken
what all hands must obey.

One ember enters
the mortal world—

and law gives way.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, quiet and exact]
Or law is asked
what purpose it protects.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
The purpose is survival.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
Whose survival?

[Silence.]

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
Bring me the forge.

From earth and water,
fashion a woman.

Call her Pandora:

all gifts gathered
in one name.

[Anvil strikes begin.]

[HEPHAESTUS — Male rough baritone, slow and heavy]
From earth and water,
I shape the form.

Not cruel.
Not broken.

A living body
made warm.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, restrained]
I give her skill.
I give her hands
to weave the thread
and guide the loom.

[APHRODITE — Female warm soprano, luminous and uneasy]
I give her beauty.
I give her longing.
I give her grace
to fill the room.

[HERMES — Male agile tenor, quick and shadowed]
I give her language.
I give her laughter.
I give her words
that open doors.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
And what is Pandora
being made for?

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
For mortals to remember
that every fire has cost.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Divided choir, ritual and accusatory]
One gave beauty.
One gave breath.
One gave language.
One hid death.

No single hand
would bear the blame.

So all of heaven
forged the shame.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, sharper]
A gift without consent
is not a gift.

A law that hears
no answering voice

will make a weapon
of its craft.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
You call it weapon.

I call it balance.

The fire has taught them
to defy the sky.

They must learn
that every opening
can also let destruction rise.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
Then let them learn
through truth.

Not through a life
you built to carry
what you will not name.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
Bring the vessel.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Low male choir, ritual]
Seal the sorrow.
Seal the fever.
Seal the hunger.
Seal the grief.

[APHRODITE — Female warm soprano, distant]
Seal the longing
that outlives relief.

[HERMES — Male agile tenor, quick and shadowed]
Seal the word
that turns trust thin.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Low male choir, ritual]
Seal the world
they carry in.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Full choir, heavier]
One gave beauty.
One gave breath.
One gave language.
One hid death.

A thousand gifts.
One hidden chain.

All of heaven
forged the pain.

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano, newly awakened, warm and confused]
Why have you given me
these hands?

Why have you placed
this weight inside them?

You call me gift.

But no one asks
what I would choose.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, almost a whisper]
I gave her skill.

I did not give her
to this use.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
Take her below.

Let mortal eyes
receive what they have earned.

[PANDORA — Female dramatic mezzo-soprano]
What have I earned?

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
The cost of fire.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
No.

The cost of fear.

[Outro: the vessel seals. One church bell. A soft voice from within:]
Open.
```
