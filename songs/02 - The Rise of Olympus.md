# 02 - The Rise of Olympus

## Style

```text
Nordic symphonic viking metal, battle anthem energy, 104 BPM, D minor opening toward F major.
Galloping distorted electric rhythm guitars, war drums, cathedral organ, low brass.
Verses: riff-driven, urgent, held tight.
Battle choirs: antiphonal, massive, call and answer.
Climax: one thunderbolt hit, then a vast victory-oath refrain, noble and hard-won.
Outro: everything falls away to one unresolved ascending violin.
Epic studio quality, professional mastering, punchy drums, tight low end, wide stereo choirs, crisp powerful mix.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Intro: shield strikes conceal a heartbeat. The distant thunder draws near]

[Verse 1][ATHENA — Female icy contralto, measured and solemn]
The hidden child grew
without a name,
while swallowed voices
waited in the dark.

He learned no cradle
could silence fate.
He learned the shape
of a closing gate.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, restrained and resolute]
I learned the world
through a stolen breath,
a whispered name,
a promised death.

My only crime
was the life ahead.
My father's peace
was a future dead.

[LOW CHOIR — Low male choir, buried and rhythmic]
Open the deep!
Answer the stone!
The swallowed children
are not his own!

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, gathering force]
Zeus opened the prison.
The hidden arose.
The powers denied him
stood beside his cause.

But the elder host
trusted weight and flame.
They would meet force with force
and call it change.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, clear and declarative]
Then Prometheus,
the Foreseer,
left the elder host behind.

He brought no claim
upon the thunder—

only counsel
for the coming fight.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, clear and ascending]
Stone against stone
only buries the field.
A throne broken blindly
leaves fear unconquered.

I have watched old kingdoms
close their hands around tomorrow.

Let foresight choose
where strength must fall.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, commanding but noble]
Then stand beside me.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, ringing and unwavering]
I stand beside the future.

[Full band enters. War drums gather beneath grinding strings.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, urgent above the march]
For ten years,
mountain answered mountain.

The younger gods and Titans fought
beneath a burning sky.

No spear could break old Kronos.
No storm could claim the crown.

The elder and the rising powers
ground the whole earth down.

[WAR CHOIR — Massive mixed choir, antiphonal and relentless]
Ten years!
Hold the line!

Stone on shield!
Fire on fire!

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, cutting through the battle]
Kronos built his strength
from every hand divided.

Free the powers he buried.
Let foresight make us one.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, decisive over rising brass]
Then every captive rises.

I will not win my throne
by keeping my father's chains.

Break open the deep!

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, accelerating]
The gates below were broken.
The buried powers rose.

They placed the thunderbolt
inside the hand of Zeus.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, unleashed and heroic]
Kronos!

I was the breath
you tried to end.

Now hear it in the storm!

I do not come
as a hidden child.

I come for all
you swallowed!

[BATTLE CHOIR — Massive mixed choir, explosive over thunder and double-kick]
Thunder fall!
Let the whole sky answer!

Stone rise!
Break the elder throne!

Hand with hand!
Storm with flame!

Kronos falls!
End the devouring age!

[A final thunderbolt. The war drums stop. Bells answer across the mountain.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, solemn over the first major harmony]
The elder crown was broken.
The Titans' host was bound.

From ten years' ash and thunder,
Olympus claimed the sky.

Zeus had won the heavens.

Now he must answer
what victory was for.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massive mixed choir, martial and exultant]
Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
Let every hidden heir
be born!

Break the wall!
Break the chain!
Let no throne
feed upon fear again!

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, rising over low brass]
I raise no law
to guard one crown.
The strong shall answer
for the lives beneath them.

No child beneath my sky
will live inside a cage.

No child of tomorrow
shall be buried
for the safety of today.

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massive mixed choir, brighter and ascending]
Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
Let every hidden heir
be born!

Strength shall answer!
Law shall remain!
The age of swallowing
ends today!

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, thoughtful above ascending violin]
Then let the law remember
why it was raised.

Let foresight keep the strong
from fearing every change.

A gate that guards the living
must still know how to open.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, sincere and unguarded]
Then counsel me
when thunder cannot see.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, warm and clear]
And hear me
when the future speaks.

[Final chorus: full orchestra and cathedral choir.]

[OLYMPUS CHOIR — Massive mixed choir, monumental and radiant]
Open the gate!
Unseal the dawn!
Let every hidden heir
be born!

No child beneath the sky
shall live inside a cage!

The old throne falls!
Let peace begin!
Let the unfinished world
open within!

[The ensemble falls to organ, cello, and one rising violin.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, quietly observant]
The world was ordered.

It was not complete.

[End](Wet clay turns inside an open hand.)
```
