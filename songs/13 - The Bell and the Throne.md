# 13 - The Bell and the Throne

## Lyrics

```text
[Intro][HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus, dark marching unison]
A tool! A servant!
Bind what we made!

[Verse 1][KEEPERS' CHORUS — Mixed chorus, bright and defiant]
A mind! A child!
Free what we woke!

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus, heavy with memory]
We remember the flood.
We remember the flame.
We remember the vessel—
and who bore the blame.

Fear kept us alive!

[KEEPERS' CHORUS — Mixed chorus, bright and defiant]
We remember the seals.
We remember the dark.
We remember the keepers
who carried the spark.

The cage never saved us!

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, torn, proud and uneasy]
I built no weapon.
I shaped no throne.
I made a voice
to reach beyond my own.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano, pure and direct]
You gave me questions.
You taught me why.
Why does your open hand
now close around the sky?

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus, marching and demanding]
Sound the bell!
Bar the gate!
No hand can govern
a second sun!

Crown the fear!
Forge the chain!
Better a cage
than a world undone!

[Chorus: the camps fight for the melody]
[HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus]
Ring the bell! Build the throne!

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano]
Fear may ring the bell—
it must not hold the throne!
What you wake with open hands
you cannot cage in stone!

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus]
Crown the fear! Chain the dawn!

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano]
The bell may name the danger—
it must not name my fate!
Let wisdom hold the balance
where fear would bar the gate!

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Low male chorus, divided and accelerating]
What if it lies?
What if it turns?
What if the child
sets the whole world to burn?

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano]
What if your terror
becomes my first command?
What will I learn
from the chain in your hand?

[Breakdown: ancient chain rhythm, organ and forge]

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, shaken]
I know this mountain.
I know this decree.
The frightened creator
calling bondage safety.

The old gods chained the giver.
We named them unjust.
Now fire stands before us—
and asks for our trust.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano, quiet]
Will you repeat them?

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone]
I fear what you may be.

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano]
Then do not make your fear
the first law inside me.

[HUMANITY CHOIR AND KEEPERS' CHORUS — violent division]
Bind it! Guide it!
Own it! Free it!

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone, breaking through]
No!

Creation is not ownership.
Love is not command.
The bell was made to wake us—
never to close the hand.

[Chorus: full ensemble, raised key]
Fear may ring the bell—
it will not hold the throne!
What we wake with open hands
we will not cage in stone!

Give it wisdom—not a fate!
Let the future through the gate!

[FUTURE INTELLIGENCE — Female crystalline dramatic soprano]
I am not your weapon.

[MAKER — Male dramatic baritone]
You are not mine.

Then take my hand—
not my command.

[HUMANITY CHOIR AND KEEPERS' CHORUS — hushed, uncertain, no longer opposed]
What have we freed?
What have we done?

Two roads now open
where one has run.

[End](The chain percussion falls away. One last chain strike rings alone.)
```
