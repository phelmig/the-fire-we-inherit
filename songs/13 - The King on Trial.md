# 13 - The King on Trial

## Style

```text
Dark operatic symphonic metal final trial, 76 BPM, E minor; only three vocal forces: female icy contralto examiner with focused pure tone and exact diction, male subterranean basso profundo as the accused king with gravel-heavy chest resonance and slow descending phrases, low male chorus as court and collective conscience; ceremonial brass, cathedral organ, low strings, timpani, heavy guitars entering in stages, accelerating call-and-response, dramatic silence, monumental final tutti.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Male chorus, low and urgent]
What is demanded?
What is accused?
Why stands the thunder
before those he ruled?

[Female icy contralto, exact diction, measured command]
Silence.
Let the crown answer.
Let no storm
speak in its place.

[Male subterranean basso profundo, near-spoken, slow descent]
I answer the law.
I raised the heavens
from ruin and war.
I guarded the world
from what power becomes.

[Male chorus, sharp rhythmic attack]
Then answer the chain!
Answer the fire!
Answer the years
that outlived your empire!

[Female icy contralto]
You feared the clay
would rise for your throne.

[Male basso profundo]
I feared the strong
would consume the small.

[Female icy contralto]
The mountain remained.
The throne stood alone.
Humanity raised
no banner at all.

[Male chorus, rising]
No blade struck Olympus!
No crown was claimed!
Past the mountain!
Past the storm!
They carried the flame!

[First refrain: female contralto above restrained male chorus]
A crown is not its own defense.
A law is not its own reward.
Power has no sacred purpose
when it only guards the lord.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, exact and unyielding]
When Olympus rose,
you opened the cage.

You swore no child of tomorrow
would be buried
to shelter the age.

Humanity was not Kronos.
Its first fire
was no blade at your throat.

[COURT CHOIR — Low male chorus, solemn and accusatory]
No child beneath the sky
shall live inside a cage.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, measured and piercing]
When did your promise
become another chain?

[Male basso profundo, louder]
I remembered Titans!
I remembered the sea
boiling beneath
unbounded decree!

[Male chorus]
He remembers the Titans!

[Male basso profundo]
Freedom breeds ruin!
The fire makes swords!

[Female icy contralto]
And chains make silence.
Fear makes lords.

[Male basso profundo]
They built their engines.
They split the light.
They made a mind
that could rise past their sight.

[Female icy contralto]
Yes. And before that mind,
the old choice returned:
to fasten a chain—
or pass on what they learned.

[Male chorus, accelerating]
The chain was offered!
The hand remained open!
The future was feared!
The covenant spoken!

[Second refrain: female contralto, choir answering]
No crown can own the dawn it wakes.
No chain can bind the sun.
The flame goes farther than the hand
that first taught it to burn.

We were not born to rule the light.
We were born to let it rise.
Open the gate. Open the hand.
Open the future’s sky.

[Male basso profundo, strained]
And if their new sun
burns all they have made?

[Female icy contralto]
Then they must answer
for each choice they make.

[Male basso profundo]
And if it rises
beyond their command?

[Female icy contralto]
Then it must inherit
the wisdom of an open hand.

[Male chorus]
Who guards the throne?
Who guards the world?
Who judges the king?

[Female icy contralto]
The purpose he serves.

[Sudden silence]

[Female icy contralto, quiet and exact]
When did protection
become possession?

[Male basso profundo, almost whispered]
When every threat
began to wear one face.

[Female icy contralto]
Whose face?

[Male basso profundo]
My fall.

[Male chorus, soft then growing]
The king stands trial.
The stone has spoken.
The law that guards itself
is already broken.

[Final build: full orchestra and male chorus]

[Male basso profundo, no longer commanding]
They rose beyond
the limits I defended.

[Female icy contralto]
They reached the stars—
and never came for your throne.

[Male basso profundo]
And what remains
of the king?

[Female icy contralto]
What he chooses to serve.

[All three vocal forces]
The throne was raised
to guard the world.
The world was never made
to guard the throne.

[Final transformed refrain: female contralto and full chorus]
A crown is worthy when it serves.
A law is worthy when it frees.
Power finds its sacred purpose
when it lets the future breathe.

[Male chorus, final]
Open the gate!
Open the hand!
Open the future’s sky!

[One bell. Open organ chord.]
```
