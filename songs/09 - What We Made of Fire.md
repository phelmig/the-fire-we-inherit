# 09 - What We Made of Fire

## Style

```text
Aggressive epic progressive symphonic power metal, 128 BPM, A minor rising into C major; riff-driven distorted electric rhythm guitars, palm-muted verse riffing, soaring overdriven electric lead guitar climbs, industrial forge percussion, pistons, turbines, launch countdowns and relentless mechanical double-kick; pipe organ, brass, soaring strings, impassioned lead vocals, soaring counter-melodies, colossal mixed gang chorus, rocket-like orchestral builds; technological exhilaration tempered by the recurring principle that fear may warn but must not rule.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Anvil strikes become pistons and turbines.]

[HUMANITY — Male dramatic baritone, commanding and visionary]
The gate stood open.
The ember endured.
We hammered the unknown
until it had form.

We raised no army
to conquer a throne.
We followed the question
where no map had gone.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Mixed gang chorus, forceful chant]
Forge the steel!
Harness the light!
Measure the heavens!
Outrun the night!

[HUMANITY — Female angelic soprano, soaring, the widening horizon]
The fire became number.
The number became code.
The code became engines
that carried our hope.

[First refrain: full choir and heavy guitars]
Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Not for a throne—
but to answer the why!

Fire to engine!
Engine to star!
The children of clay
will reach farther than gods ever are!

Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Fear may warn us—
wisdom decides!

[HUMANITY — Male dramatic baritone, accelerating]
We split the atom.
We mapped the gene.
We gave sight to metal
and memory to machines.

We bent light through lenses.
We spoke through the air.
We raised towers of steel
where no wings had been there.

We crossed the oceans
by compass and chart.
We turned the pulse of lightning
into the world’s living heart.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Mixed gang chorus, driving]
Raise the tower!
Light the core!
Count down the heavens!

Ten! Nine! Eight! Seven!

Open the door!

[Second refrain: larger]
Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Not for a throne—
but to answer the why!

Fire to engine!
Engine to star!
The children of clay
will reach farther than gods ever are!

Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Fear may warn us—
wisdom decides!

[Breakdown: alarms, slowing machinery, piano remains]

[HUMANITY — Female angelic soprano, mournful]
Every engine carries
bread or war.
Every lens can witness—
or watch from afar.

Every code can open
a door in the night—
or teach another gate
how to close it tight.

[HUMANITY — Male dramatic baritone, shaken but resolute]
We built faster than wisdom.
We reached farther than care.
We mastered the instrument—
not the purpose it should bear.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Mixed gang chorus, divided]
Build it!
Break it!
Sell it!
Save it!

[HUMANITY — Female angelic soprano, clear]
The fire is not evil.
The hand is not pure.
No answer is worthy
that fear can secure.

[HUMANITY — Male dramatic baritone, resolute]
Let fear be the bell—
not the throne.
Let it mark the cost—
not make us stone.

[Final refrain: raised key, full orchestra]
Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Not for a throne—
but to answer the why!

Fire to engine!
Engine to star!
The children of clay
have become more than gods dreamed they are!

Touch the void!
Conquer the sky!
Fear may warn us—
wisdom decides!

[HUMANITY — Male dramatic baritone, awed]
We gave steel wings.
We gave machines sight.
We built a mirror from language—

[HUMANITY — Female angelic soprano, distant answer]
—and it answered with light.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Mixed gang chorus, monumental]
Forge the future!
Open the door!

[HUMANITY — Full ensemble, monumental and resolute]
Creation now stands
where creation stood before.

[Outro: a crystalline tone rises through the engine pulse]
```
