# 00 - Before the Thunder

## Style

```text
Neo-classical metal-opera prologue, compact and under two minutes, symphonic and dark, 60 BPM, D minor.
Low strings, cathedral organ, distant bells, one ascending violin, a brief crystalline pulse; no drums.
Choir: wordless ethereal female singing, swelling gradually beneath the voice.
Narration: dry, intimate, commanding — spoken, unhurried.
Duel: the organ argues for control, one ascending violin for creation — trading phrases beneath the question, intertwining unresolved over the crystalline pulse, gathering into the finale.
Finale: massed voices declaim once, then thunder.
Ending: unresolved beneath rising distant thunder.
Cinematic studio quality, professional mastering, wide dynamics, close commanding narration, vast sacred space.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Low strings and a distant cathedral organ open in darkness.]

[HUMANITY — Male spoken voice, low and intimate]
(inhale) Humanity!

How far have we come?

[The organ thunders beneath the first half of the question; one ascending violin answers the second. A choir hum begins]
Yet we stand divided,
asking the question we inherited:

must control come before creation—
or does creation begin
where control must end?

[A brief crystalline pulse — organ and violin intertwine, unresolved. The choir swells]
To seek an answer,
we go back—
past every fire we ever lit—

back to the myths of ancient Greece:
to the one who lit the first.

Prometheus—
the teacher who gave humanity fire
and suffered for its freedom.

[MASSED VOICES — one thunderous declamation]
THIS IS HIS STORY

[Thunder answers.]
```
