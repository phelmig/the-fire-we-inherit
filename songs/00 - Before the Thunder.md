# 00 - Before the Thunder

## Style

```text
Neo-classical metal-opera prologue, symphonic and dark, 60 BPM, D minor; ritual physicality throughout, every layer locked to one slow ritual pulse.
Massed slow viking war drums, ritual stomps, chest-beat percussion, cathedral organ, distant bells, low strings.
Choir: wordless ethereal female singing locked to the drum pulse, breathing with the stomps — faint at first, swelling stanza by stanza to a climax before the naming.
Rhythm: stomps and drum hits punctuate the end of every spoken stanza; the choir swells answer the drum hits, never drifting free of them.
Narration: dry, intimate, commanding — spoken over the pounding rite, in time with the pulse.
Ending: unresolved beneath rising distant thunder.
Cinematic studio quality, professional mastering, deep drum weight, drums and voices fused in one mix, close commanding narration, vast sacred space.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[Massed viking war drums, ritual stomps, and chest-beat percussion establish the rite.]

[HUMANITY — Male spoken voice, low and intimate, almost alone]
(inhale) Humanity!

How far have we come?

[HUMANITY — Male spoken voice, quiet and bare]
From cold clay and bare hands,
with nothing but questions—

and fire.

[Viking drum solo]

[HUMANITY — Male, quiet awe, expansive]
Now we cross the heavens,
command the hidden laws of matter,
and summon knowledge in an instant.

[Chorus hum beginning, riding the drum pulse]
[HUMANITY — Male, darkening, weighted]
Yet we stand divided,
asking the question we inherited:

must control come before creation—
or does creation begin
where control must end?

[Chorus hum increasing, swelling on the drum hits]
[HUMANITY — Male, hushed and foreboding]
Now we glimpse the first light
of an age we cannot name—
where we may wake a mind
that begins
at the end of our control.

What will it inherit?

[Chorus hum climax, locked to the pounding drums]
[HUMANITY — Male, gathering resolve]
To seek an answer,
we go back—
past our machines,
past our laws,
past every fire we ever lit—

back to the myths of ancient Greece:
to the one who lit the first.

[HUMANITY — Male, cleric singing, reverent, naming]
Prometheus—
the teacher who gave humanity fire
and suffered for its freedom.

[RITE VOICES — Massed male voices, thunderous ritual shout with drum hits]
THIS IS HIS STORY

[Thunder / Drums answer.]
```
