# 00 - Before the Thunder

## Style

```text
Dark cinematic symphonic-metal prologue, 60 BPM, D minor, very slow and spacious, intimate low spoken narration rather than theatrical declamation, sub-bass drone, bowed double basses, solo cello, distant cathedral pipe organ, faint wind, and sparse anvil-like sparks, ethereal female choir gradually emerges beneath an ominous orchestral swell, no full drums or heavy guitars until a final restrained impact; end unresolved, flowing directly into 01 - Before the Throne.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[HUMANITY — Male spoken voice, low and intimate, weathered and searching]

Humanity…

How far have we come?

We cross the heavens,
command the hidden laws of matter,
and summon knowledge in an instant.

Yet we stand divided,
asking the question we inherited:
must control come before creation—
or does creation begin
where control must end?

Now we prepare to awaken a mind
that may surpass its makers
and grow beyond our plans.

What will it inherit?

To seek an answer,
we return to the myths of ancient Greece—
and to Prometheus,
the teacher who gave humanity fire
and suffered for its freedom.

This is his story.

[Thunder]

Before the thunder...
```
