# 02b - The Promise Kept

## Style

```text
Dark sacred symphonic power-metal sacrifice scene, 82 BPM, E minor; cathedral organ, church bells, low brass, anvil strikes, solo violin, rising strings, ritual male choir, heavy orchestration and double-kick at the sentence; a single hidden ember, a growing hearth, and a final chain strike; morally defiant, solemn, and tragic rather than rebellious.
```

## Lyrics

```text
[An unlit hearth. Wind through a mortal shelter.]

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto, measured]
Prometheus remembered
the promise he had made.

Below, the new-made people
knew winter, dark, and rain.

No hearth against the cold.
No lamp against the night.
No bread from grain.
No metal from stone.

No answer to the white.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
But Zeus looked down
from the guarded height.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, slow and immovable]
The fire remains
upon the height.

What mortals touch,
mortals misuse.

A spark becomes
a forge.
A forge becomes
a sword.

I will not give them
power to choose.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, rising over solo violin]
The flame is warmth
against the cold.

The flame is craft
in mortal hands.

The first bright answer
they can hold

to shape the world
they understand.

You gave them law.
I gave them life.

You made a world
where they may stand.

Do not make darkness
their condition.

Do not close
their empty hands.

[REFRAIN — High tenor above choir]
I gave them hands
to shape the world.

I gave them eyes
for dawn.

If they cannot choose
their fate,

why were they
born at all?

No hand was made
for chains!

No mind was made
to kneel!

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
Light becomes power.
Power becomes ruin.

A right without a boundary
leaves nothing safe.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor]
Then teach the cost.
Then name the wound.

But do not make
their darkness law.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
The Foreseer saw
the mountain.

He saw the chain.
He saw the eagle
circle in the rain.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, almost whispered]
I know the price.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
He saw the fire
scar the hand that bore it.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor]
I know the price.

[ATHENA — Female icy contralto]
Still he walked
toward the guarded height.

[The music falls away. One dry fennel stalk catches fire.]

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor]
No army.
No banner.
No hunger for a crown.

One ember
carried downward

where the dark
had settled down.

[HUMANITY — Female intimate alto, distant]
Warmth.

[HUMANITY CHOIR — Mixed choir, whispered]
Light.
Bread.
Iron.
Home.

[Full orchestra erupts.]

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, thunderous]
You broke my law.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor]
I kept my word.

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo]
Call this theft.
Call this wrong.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor]
I will not call
their right a crime.

[REFRAIN — Full choir]
I gave them hands
to shape the world.

I gave them eyes
for dawn.

If they cannot choose
their fate,

why were they
born at all?

No hand was made
for chains!

No mind was made
to kneel!

[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, cold decree]
Then let the mountain
keep you.

[PROMETHEUS — Male bright high lyric tenor, unwavering]
Then let it keep
my body.

Not my answer.

[One chain strike. The hearth continues.]
```
