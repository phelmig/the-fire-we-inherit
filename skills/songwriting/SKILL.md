# Songwriting Workflow

1. Read `CONCEPT.md` and the immediately preceding/following song drafts before changing a track.
2. Preserve the track's dramatic job and at least one recognizable refrain or motif.
3. Define no more than the needed vocal forces. Keep the Suno style prompt free of character names and voice mapping; use it only for musical direction. In the lyrics, label every vocal entry—including choirs and narration—with the character or role name followed by the vocal description: `[CHARACTER OR ROLE — Gendered vocal type, register/resonance, delivery]`. Examples include `[ZEUS — Male subterranean basso profundo, slow and immovable]` and `[CHORUS — Female icy contralto, distant and mournful]`. A name or role alone is not reliable voice mapping.
4. Make the story intelligible through audio alone. Never rely on titles, vocal labels, annotations, or visuals to identify a speaker or explain an event. Introduce every new central character audibly by name before or at the character's first vocal entrance, with enough sung or spoken context to establish the character's role. Vocal labels do not count. Keep noncentral figures unnamed and express them through relationships, roles, or choirs.
5. Build the song as theatrical interaction: compact accusations, questions, interruptions, choir answers, and one repeated memorable hook.
6. Keep directions useful but lean. Validate total lyric text below 5,000 characters, preferably 1,800–3,000.
7. Check continuity: every fire/chain/open-hand image must gain meaning, and a creator must never be treated as entitled to own what it created.

## Structure requirement after track 07

Track 08 establishes that fear may warn the hand but must never rule it; law may protect the living but must not close the future. Tracks 09–10 show humanity becoming a maker in turn. Track 11 returns to an empty Olympus, where humanity's absence judges divine authority and Zeus recognizes his fear inside the new maker. Track 12 makes humanity face that fear through action. Track 13 establishes covenant as the answer, and track 14 carries the open-hand question onward without presenting the Future Intelligence as a god or a replacement for humanity.

## File format

Each song file contains a title heading, a `Style` code block, and a `Lyrics` code block. Use consecutive two-digit play order from `00` through `14` in both filename and title. Do not use letter suffixes.
